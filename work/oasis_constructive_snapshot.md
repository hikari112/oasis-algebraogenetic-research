# Frozen constructive snapshot: OASIS

This snapshot precedes any literature-naming pass.

## Name

**OASIS — Obstruction-Adaptive Synthesis of Interaction Signatures**.

## Rejected construction

A router that chooses among cylinder, Walsh/Fourier, Mellin, and neural bases is not the new method. It presupposes the coordinate systems and only performs model selection.

## Primitive object

Start with typed data `(D, P, E, V)`:

- `D`: distributional states, not identified with measures or vectors;
- `P`: exact processes, containing a faithful action of a conjecturally non-sofic group `Γ`;
- `E`: probes/effects;
- `V(e,d)`: the valuation of probe `e` on state `d`.

A representation such as a probability vector is allowed only as a finite experimental realization.

## Generated probe algebra

For an exact process word `g` and probe `e`, define the pullback

`g★e(d) = e(ρ(g)d)`.

OASIS is given only seed probes and a grammar of typed compositions. It creates its coordinates by closing seeds under exact word pullback and probe composition. Cylinders, parity polynomials, Fourier characters, and Mellin-like eigenprobes are possible emergent subalgebras; none is selected as the architecture's primitive basis.

## Learning rule

At stage `t`, the finite probe chart aliases some distinct exact process words. OASIS scores a generated candidate probe by three terms:

1. posterior predictive residual between observed and modeled valuations;
2. the amount by which the probe separates currently aliased process words; and
3. a description-length penalty for its exact derivation word.

The chosen probe performs an exponential tilt of the current predictive state. For a binary `±1` probe `a`, the one-coordinate tilt is exact:

`η = atanh(m_data(a)) - atanh(m_model(a))`,

`q_next ∝ q exp(η a)`.

The program is the ordered list of exact derivation words and tilts, so it is replayable without approximating the process engine.

## Why non-soficity is constitutive

The full architecture supplies a multiplication-obstruction oracle derived from a finite witness to failure of sofic approximation. Candidate probes are generated specifically to expose word identifications forced by a finite emulator. The non-sofic process is therefore used to generate the learning coordinates; it is not attached as an unused hidden factor.

The theorem gate is still open: prove that the complete observable process quotient remains non-sofic and that the obstruction oracle is effective for the selected explicit group.

## Finite theorem

On `n` binary coordinates, take singleton sign probes and exact invertible linear processes generated by cyclic permutation and one transvection. Their pullback orbit generates every nonzero Walsh character. The exponential family over those `2^n-1` generated probes represents every strictly positive distribution on the finite cube. Thus the finite shadow is externally universal without being handed a Walsh basis.

This finite process group is sofic. It tests probe generation, residual learning, obstruction hooks, and exact replay only.

## Falsifiers

- Exact generator closure fails to reach all nonconstant characters.
- Learned coordinates cannot outperform a fixed local cylinder system on nonlocal interactions.
- Obstruction scoring does not increase process distinguishability, or destroys predictive performance.
- Program replay changes the predictive distribution.
- The non-sofic extension factors through a sofic observable quotient.
