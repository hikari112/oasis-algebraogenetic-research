## Executive collision verdict

The active cohomology atlas is a clean synthesis of standard covering-space, signed-local-system, and exact-information arguments. Its individual mathematical components are strongly collided. Adaptive representation growth and history-sensitive coalgebraic completion also have close established precedents, especially counterexample-guided abstraction refinement and dependent/history-sensitive final coalgebras.

What was **not found** is one theorem combining all three of the following:

1. an invariant, semantics-forced **causal admission law** that constructs a genuinely new observable or formation problem from a certified failure of the current language;
2. an **essential future-universal role** for that admitted object across later continuations not already encoded in the admission certificate; and
3. a non-circular lower bound against an independently defined, bounded, **composite old-construction closure** `OldSat_B`.

This remaining conjunction is an open research target, not a demonstrated novelty claim.

## 1. Character covers and the coarsest simultaneous trivializer

Let \(K\) be connected and let \(S\leq H^1(K;\mathbb F_2)\). Evaluation defines

\[
\rho_S:\pi_1(K)\longrightarrow S^*,
\qquad
\rho_S(\gamma)(\alpha)=\alpha(\gamma).
\]

The connected based cover corresponding to \(\ker\rho_S\) is regular, with deck group \(\operatorname{im}\rho_S\) (equal to \(S^*\) when the evaluations are jointly surjective). If another connected based cover \(Y\to K\) kills every class in \(S\), then its fundamental-group subgroup lies in \(\ker\rho_S\), so \(Y\to K\) factors through this cover. This is precisely the proposed "coarsest simultaneous trivializer" property, with cover-arrow orientation made explicit.

### Primary sources

- Allen Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, covering-space classification, deck transformations, and local coefficients: <https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATpage.html>
- Jonathan L. Gross and Thomas W. Tucker, "Generating All Graph Coverings by Permutation Voltage Assignments," *Discrete Mathematics* 18 (1977), 273-283. The paper proves that every graph cover is generated by a permutation voltage assignment and every regular graph cover by an ordinary voltage assignment: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0012365X77901315>
- Goulnara Arzhantseva, Erik Guentner, and Jan Spakula, "Coarse Non-Amenability and Coarse Embeddings," using iterated \(\mathbb Z/2\)-homology covers: <https://math.hawaii.edu/~erik/papers/CEnotA.pdf>
- Jeff Erickson and Amir Nayyeri, "Minimum Cuts and Shortest Non-Separating Cycles via Homology Covers," using homology covers algorithmically: <https://jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/pubs/homcover.html>

### Inference

For a basis of \(S\), the cover is the connected component of the fiber product of the corresponding character double covers. On a graph it is an elementary-abelian voltage/derived cover. For \(S=H^1(K;\mathbb F_2)\), the established name is the \(\mathbb Z/2\)-homology cover. "Coarsest simultaneous trivializer" is a useful universal-property description, but no separate established name for that description was located. A proper \(S\)-cover should not be called *characteristic* unless \(S\) is invariant under the relevant automorphism action.

## 2. Signed rank-one local systems, double covers, and Hodge untwisting

A character \(\chi:\pi_1(K)\to\{\pm1\}\) defines a flat real rank-one local system and an associated double cover. Pullback to a cover whose subgroup lies in \(\ker\chi\) trivializes the holonomy. A normalized nonzero parallel section then conjugates the twisted differential to the ordinary differential; with compatible inner products, it similarly conjugates the corresponding Laplacians.

### Primary sources

- Frank Harary, "On the Notion of Balance of a Signed Graph," *Michigan Mathematical Journal* 2 (1953-54), 143-146: <https://projecteuclid.org/journals/michigan-mathematical-journal/volume-2/issue-2/On-the-notion-of-balance-of-a-signed-graph/10.1307/mmj/1028989917.full>
- Afonso S. Bandeira, Amit Singer, and Daniel A. Spielman, "A Cheeger Inequality for the Graph Connection Laplacian," including flat/synchronized assignments and connection-Laplacian nullspaces: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.3873>
- Jakob Hansen and Robert Ghrist, "Toward a Spectral Theory of Cellular Sheaves," developing cellular-sheaf Hodge Laplacians and their relation to sheaf cohomology: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01513>
- Amit Singer and Hau-Tieng Wu, "Vector Diffusion Maps and the Connection Laplacian": <https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0075>

### Inference

In graph language, vanishing sign holonomy is balance, and changing a vertex sign is switching or gauge transformation. The active atlas's normalized untwisting is therefore standard gauge trivialization of a flat sign line. Its exact packaging with an \(H^1\)-selected simultaneous cover is useful, but the constituent geometry and operator identity are ordinary.

## 3. Exact nonlinear sufficient representations for all period queries

Suppose an arbitrary encoder \(E(x)\), without a linearity assumption, permits exact decoding of every query \(\chi(x)\) for \(\chi\in T\). If two inputs have different complete response vectors, they cannot share an encoding. If all \(2^d\) response vectors occur, the encoding range has at least \(2^d\) states and therefore requires at least \(d\) bits.

### Primary sources

- Ilan Kremer, Noam Nisan, and Dana Ron, "On Randomized One-Round Communication Complexity," *STOC 1995* / *Computational Complexity* 8 (1999), for one-way communication lower-bound machinery: <https://doi.org/10.1145/225058.225277>
- Anil Nerode, "Linear Automaton Transformations," *Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society* 9 (1958), 541-544, the source of the future-indistinguishability/minimal-state viewpoint: <https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1958-0135681-9>
- Ashwin Nayak, "Optimal Lower Bounds for Quantum Automata and Random Access Codes," for the approximate/quantum random-access-code extension: <https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9904093>
- J. W. Carlyle and Azaria Paz, "Realizations by Stochastic Finite Automata," for finite-rank/linear realization connections: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022000071800053>

### Inference

The exact deterministic lower bound in the atlas is the elementary injectivity of the response-vector quotient. It is also deterministic one-way INDEX/inner product and Myhill-Nerode future distinguishability. Coordinate queries already force the bound; a randomized communication theorem or Hankel-rank theorem is unnecessary. Hankel rank is relevant only after restricting the representation/decoder class to a linear or weighted realization.

### Inference

These precedents normally refine within a predeclared schema, predicate language, feature universe, refinement calculus, or uniformly specified meta-generator. Consequently, adaptation or on-demand materialization alone is not the residue. The new burden would be to show that the admitted observable is outside every bounded composite use of the old formation grammar, rather than merely a newly selected member of it.

## 5. History-indexed guarded coalgebras and completion

Final coalgebras already provide unique behavior maps and coiteration for all coalgebras of a fixed functor. Guarded recursion, indexed/fibred coalgebra, final sequences, and completion of initial structures are established extensions. More decisively, prior work already considers interaction possibilities that depend on prior interaction history.

### Primary sources

- Jan J. M. M. Rutten, "Universal Coalgebra: A Theory of Systems," *Theoretical Computer Science* 249 (2000), 3-80: <https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/48/>
- Peter Hancock and Anton Setzer, "Interactive Programs and Weakly Final Coalgebras in Dependent Type Theory." Their second interface form explicitly allows potential interactions to depend on the history of previous interactions: <https://academic.oup.com/book/5354/chapter-abstract/148148869>
- Vincenzo Ciancia, Alexander Kurz, and Ugo Montanari, "Symmetries, Local Names and Dynamic (De)-Allocation of Names," introducing accessible functors for **history-dependent automata** and proving final-coalgebra existence in a broad class: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ic.2009.10.007>
- James Worrell, "On the Final Sequence of a Finitary Set Functor," *Theoretical Computer Science* 338 (2005): <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304397504008023>
- Lars Birkedal, Rasmus E. Mogelberg, Jan Schwinghammer, and Kristian Stovring, "First Steps in Synthetic Guarded Domain Theory": <https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.3596>
- Neil Ghani, Patricia Johann, and Clement Fumex, "Indexed Induction and Coinduction, Fibrationally": <https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.3263>
- Adriana Balan and Alexander Kurz, "On Coalgebras over Algebras," including final-coalgebra-as-completion results: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.0938>

### Inference

"History-dependent final coalgebra" is already collided terminology and should not be used as a novelty label. History dependence, guardedness, arbitrarily deep refinement, and universal coiteration do not distinguish genesis if the changing interface is generated by one fixed dependent polynomial or accessible functor. Finality itself supplies a future-universal role for a fixed functor; the possible novelty burden is the endogenous, invariant admission of the functor/observable grammar and its separation from all bounded old composites.

## Exact surviving residue

The strongest defensible target is the conjunction below.

### A. Causal admission

A certified failure of the current observable language canonically forces a new observable or formation problem. The result must be invariant under presentation changes, gauge, replay, stuttering, and equivalent certificate encodings. It must not be a hidden stage counter, workload schedule, or arbitrary choice among equivalent candidates.

### B. Future-parametric essentiality

After admission, the new object must be essential for a family of genuinely later continuations that was not explicitly enumerated in the admission certificate. "Universal for all coalgebras of an already fixed functor" is standard and does not meet this requirement by itself.

### C. Composite old-closure separation

For an independently specified cost model and closure,

\[
\forall B\;\exists n\;\forall C\in\operatorname{OldSat}_B(\Xi_0),
\qquad C\not\simeq_{\mathrm{fut}}G_n.
\]

Here `OldSat_B` must be closed under all relevant bounded compositions, including covers, sign local systems, fixed and generated predicates, materialized views, CEGAR-style refinement, query learners, compilers, final-sequence stages, guarded or pro-completion, replay, caching, and bounded nonuniform advice. The equivalence \(\simeq_{\mathrm{fut}}\) must compare the future role, not merely current point evaluations.

## Principal falsifier

Assume the admission mechanism can be expressed as one fixed computable indexed operation

\[
G(h,\mathrm{certificate})=\text{next observable or next interface}.
\]

If the old grammar can contain \(G\), its compiler, or a bounded universal interpreter for it, then dependent coalgebra, CEGAR, or ordinary generated-view machinery may absorb the whole proposal. Excluding \(G\) from `OldSat_B` merely by definition would make the separation circular.

A genuine result therefore needs all of the following:

1. `OldSat_B` and its cost model fixed independently of the desired witness;
2. closure under reasonable changes of presentation, compilation, bounded advice, and composite reuse;
3. a lower bound against every member of that closure;
4. a constructive upper bound for the admitted object or atlas;
5. an essential-future-use witness; and
6. robustness under replay, stuttering, gauge, and equivalent obstruction certificates.

Failure of any one of these conditions is a serious collapse result, not merely an implementation defect.

## Probability and optimization boundary

The carrier in this calibration is deterministic: covers, local systems, response quotients, and coalgebraic behavior do not require probability. Probability can assign states or weights to the admitted observable algebra, and optimization can prioritize which certificate or refinement to pursue, but neither supplies the missing admission theorem. Existing adaptive systems show that optimization-driven refinement is common; the unresolved issue is invariant formation and bounded composite-closure separation.

## Bottom line

The active cohomology atlas should be retained as an exact standard-mathematics calibration and executable testbed. Its value is precision, not novelty. The serious frontier is narrower and harder: prove that a semantics-forced admission rule creates an object with an essential future role that cannot be reproduced, up to that role, by any bounded composite of the old grammar. Until that theorem survives the principal falsifier, the broader genesis direction remains a disciplined conjectural program rather than a new field theorem.
