## 1. The mechanism that was actually searched

The target is not any one of deformation theory, persistence, holonomy,
torsors, conservative extension, or syntax generation. It is the following
composite.

Given a current observable doctrine \(\mathcal O\) and an unresolved,
presentation-invariant certificate \(\omega\):

1. construct from \((\mathcal O,\omega)\), without selecting from a
   pre-enumerated branch list, a deformation or refinement neighborhood;
2. identify the origin-free direction that survives all admissible
   refinements;
3. intersect it with the direction enacted by independently retained causal
   paths, rather than by gauge changes or arbitrary automorphisms;
4. freely and conservatively represent the resulting torsor or action
   groupoid as a new observable, without choosing a point, origin, or basis;
5. let that enlarged observable doctrine change the category of questions
   available to subsequent continuation; and
6. index repetition by causal dependency and semantic stuttering, not by an
   externally supplied stage counter.

The last feedback arrow is essential. A completed algebra containing every
eventual symbol is not the same object: it has already installed the future
distinctions whose genesis is at issue.

## 2. What the live literature audit found

The Bright Data sweep used eight mechanism-level search angles: semantic

No searched source implemented the complete six-part mechanism above. That
statement is limited to this corpus and these formulations. It is not evidence
that no such source exists.

Those are serious collision risks. Any claimed theorem must separate the new
operator from them by a universal property, not by terminology.

### 2.2 The precise residue

After known ingredients are removed, the residual proposal is:

> **Selection-to-signature feedback.** The unresolved process itself selects
> an unpointed action object that is neither merely persistent nor merely
> reachable. A universal conservative extension makes exactly that object
> representable, and its representation changes the legal signature from
> which later failures and refinements are formed.

The proposed novelty is therefore not a new torsor, cohomology group,
completion, or holonomy invariant. It would be a new **law of admission**.

An unpublished or lightly referenced manuscript using the general phrase
“generative theories” was also found in the corpus. It discusses evolving
theories through conservative extensions and semantic colimits, but the
version inspected did not supply an obstruction selector, a
persistence-versus-causal-holonomy test, or an origin-free universal torsor
admission. This is a terminology and thematic collision worth monitoring, not
a demonstrated mathematical collision.

## 3. A typed candidate for the law

Let \(\mathsf{Proc}\) be a category or \((\infty,1)\)-category of
path-retaining semantic processes. A process \(C\) contains:

- an old observable doctrine \(\mathcal O_C\);
- a declared gauge subgroupoid;
- a separately typed causal path groupoid;
- a finite unresolved certificate \(\omega\); and
- a solution or lift groupoid \(\mathcal L_C\).

The distinction between gauge and causal path is load-bearing. Quotienting
every retained loop as gauge can erase exactly the transport that is supposed
to warrant admission.

Suppose the certificate functorially generates a category
\(\operatorname{Def}(C,\omega)\) of admissible refinements and maps
\(\mathcal L_a\to\mathcal L_C\). Define the persistent core provisionally by

\[
P_{C,\omega}
=
\bigcap\nolimits^h_{a\in\operatorname{Def}(C,\omega)}
\operatorname{EssIm}(\mathcal L_a\longrightarrow\mathcal L_C).
\tag{1}
\]

Here the homotopy intersection is schematic. A theorem must specify the
ambient category, the existence of essential images, and the required
smallness or descent hypotheses.

Let \(\mathcal P_{C,\omega}\) be the retained causal path groupoid. Its loops
act on \(\mathcal L_C\). Write \(\Delta(P_{C,\omega})\) for the translations
or self-equivalences of the persistent core generated by differences among
its objects. A first nonlinear selector is the homotopy pullback

\[
J_{C,\omega}
=
\Delta(P_{C,\omega})
\mathop{\times}\limits^h_{\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal L_C)}
\operatorname{im}\left(
\Pi_1\mathcal P_{C,\omega}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal L_C)
\right).
\tag{2}
\]

In an affine linear example, (2) reduces to

\[
J_{C,\omega}
=
\Delta(P_{C,\omega})\cap H_{C,\omega},
\qquad
H_{C,\omega}=\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{Hol}_{C,\omega}).
\tag{3}
\]

Equation (2) should not be trusted as a final definition until all arrows are
typed. If the selected locus is not one torsor, its action groupoid is useful
bookkeeping:

\[
P_{C,\omega}//J_{C,\omega},
\tag{4}
\]

It is not, by itself, the admitted observable. When \(P\) is a free transitive
\(J\)-set, the homotopy quotient \(P//J\) is contractible and therefore forgets
the very translation symmetry being retained. The correctly typed datum is
the action functor \(BJ\to\mathcal S\) with value \(P\), equivalently the
principal bundle and its classifying map

\[
P_{C,\omega}
\longrightarrow
P_{C,\omega}/J_{C,\omega}
\longrightarrow
BJ_{C,\omega}.
\tag{4a}
\]

The anchor in \(BJ\), not merely the unstructured quotient groupoid, retains
the origin-change symmetries. No global section or labeled endpoint is
adjoined.

The proposed operation

\[
\mathsf{Adm}_\omega(\mathcal O_C)
\tag{5}
\]

is initial among conservative process extensions of \(\mathcal O_C\) in which
the structured \(J\)-action (4a) is representable. It must satisfy at least:

1. **no origin:** it represents the torsor action or principal bundle, never a
   chosen element, section, or basis;
2. **old conservativity:** every sentence in the old signature has the same
   validity before and after admission;
3. **process naturality:** presentation changes, chart changes, policy
   equivalences, and gauge-equivalent certificates induce equivalent
   admissions;
4. **causal sensitivity:** changing the retained path doctrine may change
   \(J\), while merely changing coordinates may not;
5. **semantic stutter:** admission is equivalent to the identity when \(J\)
   is contractible, zero, or already representable;
6. **counter-free recursion:** later admissions are indexed by causal
   dependency in the enlarged doctrine, not by a hidden integer stage;
7. **independent confluence:** causally independent admissions commute up to
   specified coherent equivalence; and
8. **strictness:** at least one finite instance proves that the extension is
   not definitional, Morita-trivial, or recoverable by the declared bounded old
   observer class.

Items (1)--(5) are a proposed specification. No construction satisfying all
eight items is proved here.

## 4. The Beth-definability dilemma

The literature audit exposes a sharper obstruction than a missing
implementation detail.

Suppose a new observable symbol is added to an old static logic.

1. If every old model admits a unique expansion, and the logic has the
   relevant Beth definability property, then the new symbol is explicitly
   definable in the old language. It was not genuinely born.
2. If the new object is an old definable quotient, subsort, product, or
   equivalent coding, the extension may be a definitional or Morita extension.
   It changes presentation rather than semantic expressive power.
3. If an old model admits many expansions forming a torsor, the extension may
   be conservative and genuinely unpointed, but the old static semantics does
   not determine which expansion is intended.

This is the **Beth--Morita--torsor trilemma** for the present proposal. See
[Petria--Diaconescu on Beth definability in institutions](https://doi.org/10.2178/jsl/1154698588)
for the institution-level setting.

The most coherent escape is not to weaken rigor. It is to enlarge the semantic
carrier from static models to path-retaining processes. Two processes may
have isomorphic static reducts while possessing different causal histories
and therefore different actions on the same persistent torsor. The process
can force an origin-free action object even when the static reduct cannot.

A decisive theorem should therefore work in a history-enriched
causal-refinement category and exhibit a forgetful functor

\[
U:\mathsf{Proc}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Mod}(\mathcal O)
\tag{6}
\]

and processes \(X,Y\) such that

\[
U(X)\simeq U(Y)
\quad\text{with identical behavior for every old observer,}
\quad\text{but}\quad
J(X)\not\simeq J(Y).
\tag{7}
\]

Equation (7) would show that no old static formula or old definable subquotient
can recover \(J\): any such construction would take isomorphic,
old-observationally equivalent reducts to equivalent outputs. At the same time,
\(J\) may be functorially forced by the enriched histories without selecting
an origin. One must then prove that \(\mathsf{Adm}\) is nevertheless initial
and conservative in the **process** category. Without both halves, “semantics
forced” either collapses to Beth definability or becomes an arbitrary choice.

This dilemma is a substantive result of the audit. It narrows the foundational
claim to process semantics rather than static model theory.

Open-map semantics is an especially close warning at this point. It already
formalizes histories through a chosen path category and can derive a logic
characteristic for the resulting bisimulation. Thus (7) is not interesting
merely because histories distinguish processes that an endpoint forgets. The
strict residue is that the path category and its modalities must themselves be
extended by the persistent causal certificate. If a fixed open-map path logic
already expresses every admitted \(BJ\)-anchored action object, the proposed
genesis step collapses into established process semantics.

## 5. Exact finite selector calibration

The finite search separates three notions that coincided in the original
eight-state example:

- formal persistence \(P\);
- transport enacted by retained paths \(H\); and
- the admissible selector \(J=P\cap H\).

### 5.1 Marked elementary-abelian sign towers

The search objects are marked triples

\[
Q=(C_2)^r,\qquad
c\in H^2(Q;\mathbf F_2),\qquad
\chi\in H^1(Q;\mathbf F_2),
\tag{8}
\]

where \(c\) classifies a central extension and \(\chi\) supplies the cyclic
sign tower. Put

\[
d_\chi=Sq^1+\chi\smile-,
\qquad
P=\ker(d_\chi:H^2\to H^3).
\tag{9}
\]

Only liftable triples satisfying \(d_\chi(c)=0\) are retained. The actual
inner-path directions are not chosen as an arbitrary subspace. They are
derived from the extension commutator pairing

\[
B_c(q,x)=c(q,x)+c(x,q),
\qquad
L_c=\operatorname{im}(B_c:Q\to H^1),
\qquad
H=d_\chi(L_c).
\tag{10}
\]

The executed exact census is:

| rank \(r\) | marked triples with \(0<\dim H<\dim P\) |
|---:|---:|
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 21 |
| 4 | 105 |

The census is the finite shadow of a short family-level no-go. After a change
of coordinates take \(\chi=x_1\). The derivation
\(h=\partial/\partial x_1\) satisfies
\(h d_\chi+d_\chi h=1\), so the twisted complex is contractible. In degree one
and two,

\[
d_\chi(x_1)=0,\qquad
d_\chi(x_j)=x_j^2+x_1x_j\quad(j>1),
\]

so

\[
P=\operatorname{im}d_\chi^1
=
\left\langle x_j^2+x_1x_j: j>1\right\rangle
\]

has dimension \(r-1\). Every nonzero liftable class can be written
\(c=d_\chi(\ell)\) with \(\ell\notin\langle\chi\rangle\). Its commutator image
is \(\langle\chi,\ell\rangle\), and applying \(d_\chi\) gives
\(H=\langle c\rangle\). Its inert radical is

\[
\ker\chi\cap\ker\ell,
\]

of dimension \(r-2\). Thus for \(r\ge3\) every strict fixture in this entire
elementary-abelian sign family is reducible, and its marked count is

\[
(2^r-1)(2^{r-1}-1).
\]

This gives \(21\) and \(105\) at ranks three and four. At rank two,
\(\dim P=\dim H=1\), so there is no proper selector. The executable audits the
first three ranks; the displayed general statement is the accompanying
linear-algebra proof, not an unbounded machine enumeration.

The smallest strict fixture is

\[
Q=\langle u,v,w\rangle,\qquad
\chi=u+v,\qquad
c=uv.
\tag{11}
\]

Its extension group is \(D_8\times C_2\), and

\[
P
=
\left\langle
uv,\;
w^2+(u+v)w
\right\rangle,
\qquad
H=\langle uv\rangle.
\tag{12}
\]

Thus

\[
0<\dim H=1<\dim P=2.
\tag{13}
\]

The \(uv\) direction is both persistent and enacted by concrete inner loops.
The second direction is persistent but path-inert, so (3) selects only
\(\langle uv\rangle\). This is the first proper-selector calibration: the
causal test removes a formally persistent direction.

It is not yet the desired irreducible example. The \(w\)-axis is an inert
radical:

\[
\chi(w)=0,\qquad
B_c(w,-)=0,\qquad
c(w,w)=0.
\tag{14}
\]

The group and the selector split into the active \(D_8\) plane and a
path-inert \(C_2\) factor. The family-level calculation above shows that every
higher-rank strict fixture has a nonzero inert radical. Moreover, for nonzero
\(\chi\) the twisted complex in this family is exact in positive degree, so

\[
\ker d_\chi^2/\operatorname{im}d_\chi^1=0.
\tag{15}
\]

The fixture proves that persistence and enacted holonomy can differ. It does
not produce an irreducible nonexact admission germ.

### 5.2 A non-elementary \(D_8\) nonexact stutter

The complementary test leaves the elementary-abelian quotient family. Use

\[
H^*(D_8;\mathbf F_2)
\cong
\mathbf F_2[a,b,w]/(a^2+ab),
\qquad
|a|=|b|=1,\quad |w|=2,
\tag{16}
\]

with sign character \(\chi=b\). Then

\[
d_b=Sq^1+b\smile-,
\qquad
d_b(a)=d_b(b)=0,
\tag{17}
\]

and in degree two

\[
d_b(a^2)=a^2b,\qquad
d_b(b^2)=b^3,\qquad
d_b(w)=0.
\tag{18}
\]

Consequently,

\[
P=\ker d_b^2=\langle w\rangle,
\qquad
\operatorname{im}d_b^1=0,
\qquad
H^2(H^*(D_8;\mathbf F_2),d_b)=\langle w\rangle\ne0.
\tag{19}
\]

The class \(w\) is represented by the central extension
\(D_{16}\to D_8\) and has compatible \(D_{32},D_{64},\ldots\) lifts. It is
therefore a genuine persistent nonexact direction. The correctly typed
fixed-base inner loop comes from \(r^2\in Z(D_8)\): conjugation by its lift
\(R^2\in D_{32}\) covers the identity on \(D_8\), and its discrepancy cochain
for the standard section is \(\lambda(i,j)=j=b(i,j)\). It realizes

\[
d_b(b)=0.
\tag{20}
\]

All vertical inner conjugations arise from the preimage of \(Z(D_8)\); modulo
kernel-gauge coboundaries their discrepancy classes span only
\(\langle b\rangle\). A reflection conjugation is not a loop in the fixed-base
fiber—it moves the base by an inner automorphism—unless a separate pullback or
homotopy-quotient construction is added.

Hence

\[
P=\langle w\rangle,\qquad H=0,\qquad J=P\cap H=0.
\tag{21}
\]

The stable core can be viewed as an unpointed two-point \(P\)-torsor, and
coefficient inversion swaps its two points. Persistence supplies no canonical
origin and no retained causal transport. The proposed admission law must
therefore **stutter**, despite the nonzero twisted cohomology.

The executable checks the integral signed carry

\[
F_\infty((i,j),(k,\ell))
=
\frac{
i+(-1)^j k-
\bigl(i+(-1)^j k\bmod 4\bigr)
}{4}.
\tag{22}
\]

It verifies all 512 twisted cocycle equations and the low-degree cochain sector
needed for (19), along with the fixed-base \(R^2\) loop calculation. It does
**not** execute the stronger statement that coefficient inversion swaps the
two persistent affine points; that statement remains theorem-derived and is
flagged false in the certificate boundary. A minimality observation among
nonzero-twist groups of order below eight is intentionally not promoted to a
theorem here.

### 5.3 Fixed-base inner-loop no-go

The attempt to find an irreducible **nonexact** positive selector in the same
scalar-sign setting is not merely unsuccessful. It is impossible.

Let

\[
\beta_\chi:H^n(Q;\mathbf F_2)\longrightarrow H^{n+1}(Q;\mathbf F_2)
\]

be the reduced Bockstein for
\(0\to\mathbf Z_\chi\xrightarrow{2}\mathbf Z_\chi\to\mathbf F_2\to0\).
For \(q\in Z(Q)\), the central-loop bar prism gives an integral
degree-minus-one operator \(K_q\) satisfying

\[
\delta K_q+K_q\delta
=
\bigl(1-(-1)^{\chi(q)}\bigr)\operatorname{id}
=
2\chi(q)\operatorname{id}.
\]

Reducing the prism mod two gives \(i_q\). Lifting a mod-two cocycle, applying
the displayed homotopy, and dividing its coboundary by two yields the
cohomology identity

\[
\boxed{
\beta_\chi i_q+i_q\beta_\chi
=
\chi(q)\operatorname{id}.
}
\tag{23}
\]

This is an identity on cohomology; the preceding integral formula is the
cochain-homotopy statement. It requires centrality of \(q\), not \(q^2=1\).

For a normalized central extension with factor class \(c\), conjugation by a
lift of \(q\in Z(Q)\) is a fixed-base loop with discrepancy

\[
\lambda_q(x)=c(q,x)+c(x,q),
\qquad
[\lambda_q]=i_q[c].
\]

If \(c\) is liftable, \(\beta_\chi[c]=0\), and (23) gives

\[
\boxed{
\beta_\chi[\lambda_q]=\chi(q)[c].
}
\tag{24}
\]

Therefore:

- if \(\chi\) vanishes on \(Z(Q)\), every fixed-base inner-loop direction is
  zero;
- if some retained central \(q\) has \(\chi(q)=1\), then \(i_q\) contracts the
  entire twisted-Bockstein complex, so
  \(\ker\beta_\chi/\operatorname{im}\beta_\chi=0\) in every degree; and
- for one extension class \(c\), actual fixed-base inner holonomy is always
  either zero or \(\langle[c]\rangle\).

Hence positive actual inner holonomy and nonzero twisted-Bockstein homology
are mutually exclusive under the current scalar-sign, central-extension,
fixed-base-inner-loop definitions. The previously requested conjunction

\[
0<\dim H<\dim P,
\qquad
\ker\beta_\chi^2/\operatorname{im}\beta_\chi^1\ne0
\]

cannot occur, before irreducibility is even considered.

An exact normalized-bar census over \(D_8,Q_8\), named order-sixteen
dihedral/quaternion/semidihedral/modular families and related presentations,
plus selected order-thirty-two families, corroborated the theorem. It was not
a complete order-thirty-two census and is not needed for the general proof.

This no-go is scoped. It does not cover noncentral kernels, matrix-valued
local systems, outer or base-moving paths retained through a homotopy
quotient, or higher/2-group extensions.

### 5.4 First matrix and base-moving escape tests

The no-go identifies the hypotheses that must change; it does not guarantee
that the first changed example succeeds. Three small, nonspectator tests fail
for three different exact reasons.

**Quarter-turn \(C_4\).** Let \(L=\mathbf Z^2\), let the generator act by

\[
J=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad V=L/2L.
\]

The matrix-local-system executable constructs the normalized bar complexes,
the two \(V\)-extensions, their affine conjugation actions, and the integral
periodic comparison. Its certificate records

\[
\dim H^{1,2,3}(C_4;V)=(1,1,1),
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(\beta^1,\beta^2)=(0,1).
\]

The constructed degree-zero complex additionally gives
\(\dim H^0(C_4;V)=1\) and \(\operatorname{rank}\beta^0=1\).

The map \(\beta^2:H^2(C_4;V)\to H^3(C_4;L)\cong\mathbf Z/2\) is an
isomorphism. Hence only the split class lifts mod four and
\(P=H=J=0\). This is not caused by an invariant spectator: \(V\) is
indecomposable and has no invariant direct complement.

**Square-lattice \(D_8\).** Let \(r\) act by \(J\) and \(s\) by
\(F=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\). An exact normalized-bar calculation gives

\[
\dim H^{0,1,2,3}(D_8;V)=(1,2,3,4),
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(\beta^0,\beta^1,\beta^2)=(1,1,2).
\]

There is one nonzero liftable degree-two class, the reduction of
\(H^2(D_8;L)\cong C_2\), and it persists through \(L/2^n\). A concrete

## 7. The next theorem package

## 8. Conclusion

The new direction was not found as a complete named construction in the
mechanism-level sweep. Its parts are not new, and two of its nearest shadows
are powerful enough that a loose statement would collapse immediately into
known mathematics.

What survives the collision audit is precise:

\[
\boxed{
\text{failure-selected refinement}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{persistent causal action object}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{unpointed universal admission}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{new future signature}.
}
\]

The finite controls now show why both adjectives in “persistent causal” are
necessary. \(D_8\times C_2\) contains a persistent direction that no retained
path enacts. The non-elementary \(D_8\) tower contains a nonexact persistent
class but no enacted direction at all. Neither licenses admission.

The central-loop prism then closes the old search space completely: within
scalar sign coefficients and fixed-base inner conjugation, enacted transport
and residual twisted-Bockstein homology cannot coexist. This is not a reason
to enlarge the same census. It is a theorem forcing the next experiment into
matrix-valued local systems, base-moving/outer path groupoids, or higher
extensions.

The first matrix probes sharpen that instruction. Quarter-turn \(C_4\) has no
nonsplit liftable class; square-lattice \(D_8\) has one, but central \(-I\)
contracts away every residual direction; and center-free \(A_2/S_3\) loses
all 2-primary cohomology to its odd normal subgroup. These failures do not
collapse the proposed operator into known mathematics. They isolate the next
mathematical question: whether a genuinely outer, path-enriched action can
enact only part of a persistent torsor without choosing an origin or
exhausting the residual obstruction.

The honest conclusion is therefore stronger than “nothing was found” and
weaker than “a new field was discovered”:

> The full feedback operator remains apparently unmatched, the finite selector
> has passed its first nontrivial controls, and the scalar fixed-base route has
> been closed by a general no-go theorem. The first three nonspectator matrix
> routes also fail for distinct exact reasons. The project should proceed by
> proving path-Beth separation and testing the \(A_4/A_3\) outer-path
> candidate, while continuing to treat novelty as an open classification
> question.
