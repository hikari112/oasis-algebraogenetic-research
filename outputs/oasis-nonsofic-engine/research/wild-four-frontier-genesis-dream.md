# QUARANTINED DREAM II: The Polarized Resolution-Wake

**This entire document is an intentionally unrestrained mathematical fantasy. It is not a proposed proof of anything. It is not a claim that the structure is consistent, definable, useful, or new. Some of its most attractive statements are probably beautiful lies.**

## One structure, not four analogies

Imagine one foundational object called a **Polarized Resolution-Wake**, written

\[
\mathfrak W.
\]

It is not a space with points already installed. It is not an algebra with all observables already installed. It is not a dynamical system evolving in a pre-given time parameter. It is a locally executable rule that responds to an unresolved distinction by generating:

- the space in which that distinction can be asked;
- the full family of its possible primitives;
- the relations among those primitives;
- the paths along which those relations can be transported;
- a polarization measuring the cost of transporting them;
- and the next unresolved distinctions exposed by the act of resolution.

The wake is the geometry left behind by exactification. The path generates its own water.

Only a finite root germ and a continuation law are given. Stages, dimensions, topology, observables, metric, and completion are generated rather than pre-installed.

The dream is that Navier–Stokes, Hodge, Collatz, and Riemann could each be translated into the nonexistence of one special pathology inside a suitably polarized resolution-wake: a class that is exactly resolvable at every finite horizon but has no bounded coherent global primitive.

Call such a pathology a **phantom wake**.

## Primitive data

A currently materialized face of \(\mathfrak W\) is a finite package

\[
S=
\bigl(
\mathcal Q_S,\,
\mathcal C_S,\,
d_S,\,
\mathcal G_S,\,
\mathscr A_S,\,
\Pi_S
\bigr).
\]

The pieces are deliberately overgeneral.

- \(\mathcal Q_S\) is the finite algebra or complex of questions currently meaningful.
- \(\mathcal C_S\) is the finite complex in which local answers and chains can be expressed.
- \(d_S\) is an exactness operator. Depending on the incarnation it might resemble a differential, a boundary, a coboundary, a transition rule, or a logical discharge operation.
- \(\mathcal G_S\) is the gauge groupoid of presentations that mean the same thing now.
- \(\mathscr A_S\) is the admissibility law saying which unresolved certificates are allowed to cause continuation.
- \(\Pi_S\) is a polarization: an energy, height, intersection form, positivity form, or spectral action on defects and transports.

The root germ \(S_0\) is finite. There is no external integer \(n\) supplied to the continuation law. This does not magically eliminate clocks: rank or size may still reveal how many births occurred. The stronger dreamed requirement is **defect causality**:

\[
\mathfrak D(S)=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Gamma(S)\simeq S.
\]

If the admitted obstruction is erased, genesis must stutter. The transition must also descend under semantic equivalence:

\[
S\simeq T
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Gamma(S)\simeq\Gamma(T),
\]

and it must operate on legal off-orbit states, not only on the canonical family it happened to generate.

## The primitive tower

For a question \(z\in\mathcal Q_S\) and a finite observational horizon \(h\), form the entire groupoid of admissible local primitives:

\[
\mathscr P_S(z;h)
=
\left[
\left\{
p:\ d_Sp=z
\text{ on every test visible through }h
\right\}
\big/\mathcal G_S
\right].
\]

No primitive is selected. All primitives, changes of primitive, relations among changes, and higher coherences are retained.

The tower

\[
\mathscr P_S(z;1)
\longleftarrow
\mathscr P_S(z;2)
\longleftarrow
\mathscr P_S(z;3)
\longleftarrow\cdots
\]

is the **resolution profile** of \(z\).

At every finite horizon this tower may be nonempty and explicitly computable. Nevertheless, there may be no coherent section through all horizons whose polarization remains bounded.

Define the fantasy defect profile

\[
\delta_S(z,h)
=
\inf_{p\in\mathscr P_S(z;h)}
\Pi_S(p).
\]

Finite realizability says

\[
\delta_S(z,h)<\infty
\qquad
\text{for every finite }h.
\]

Bounded global realizability would require a compatible choice \(p_h\) with

\[
\sup_h\Pi_S(p_h)<\infty.
\]

A phantom wake class has the first property and lacks the second.

The phrase “choice \(p_h\)” is already suspicious because the whole point is to avoid selecting a coordinate representative. A more honest version would demand a bounded equivariant section over the torsor or higher groupoid of all splittings. The dream uses both formulations and hopes they someday reconcile.

## The resolution-wake operator

Let \(\mathfrak D(S)\) be the full groupoid of minimally unresolved admissible defects in \(S\). “Full groupoid” is meant to block an arbitrary selector: if symmetry moves one defect into another, the entire orbit is attached.

For each defect \(z\), take the nerve of its primitive tower:

\[
\mathsf{Wake}_S(z)
=
N\!\left(
\int_h\mathscr P_S(z;h)
\right).
\]

The integral sign here is intentionally type-unstable. It is dreaming of a construction that assembles all finite primitive groupoids without pretending that a bounded global primitive already exists.

The successor is the universal attachment

\[
\Gamma(S)
=
S
\underset{\operatorname{ev}}{\cup}
\mathsf{Wake}_S\!\bigl(\mathfrak D(S)\bigr).
\]

It does not adjoin a chosen answer. It adjoins a representing object for the entire geometry of possible answers.

The current defect becomes exact:

\[
j_S(\mathfrak D(S))
=
d_{\Gamma(S)}P_S
\]

for a universal primitive object \(P_S\).

But exactification creates relations among primitives. Those relations become new questions:

\[
\mathcal Q_{\Gamma(S)}
=
\operatorname{Syz}\!\left(
\mathscr P_S(\mathfrak D(S))
\right)
\oplus
\Omega_S.
\]

Here

\[
\Omega_S
=
d_{\Gamma(S)}j_S-j_Sd_S
\]

is the **genesis–Stokes defect**: the failure of exactification to commute with the old boundary law.

Thus the one master recurrence is

\[
\boxed{
\text{question}
\longrightarrow
\text{full primitive geometry}
\longrightarrow
\text{universal exactification}
\longrightarrow
\text{syzygy plus genesis–Stokes defect}
\longrightarrow
\text{new question}.
}
\]

Vertical and horizontal genesis are projections of this recurrence.

- Vertical genesis sees the higher coherences in the primitive geometry.
- Horizontal genesis sees loops and syzygies among primitives as new ordinary transport.
- Oblique genesis sees \(\Omega_S\), where the meaning of degree or boundary changes during attachment.

The dream refuses to lower a high-degree obstruction directly into a new \(\pi_1\) bit. Instead, ordinary transport could arise as

\[
H_1\!\left(\mathsf{Wake}_S(z)\right),
\]

the circulation among all ways of resolving the higher obstruction.

The band becomes a road because its moduli space has loops, not because someone renamed its degree.

## Resolution respiration

The wake breathes:

\[
Q_k
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Prim}}
P_k
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Exact}}
0
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Rel}}
Q_{k+1}.
\]

The zero is deceptive. The old question vanishes as a cohomology class, but its semantic content migrates into the ambiguity and relations of its primitive.

Dream a conservation law:

\[
\mathfrak U(S)
=
\sum_r(-1)^r
\Pi_S\!\left(
\text{questions}_r
-\text{primitives}_r
+\text{syzygies}_r
\right).
\]

Exactification does not destroy unresolvedness. It transports unresolvedness across degree:

\[
\mathfrak U(\Gamma(S))
=
\mathfrak U(S)
-\mathsf{Diss}_S
+\mathsf{Flux}_S.
\]

The dissipation term is nonnegative. The flux term measures unresolvedness crossing the current observational boundary.

This is the fantasy’s common source of viscosity, Hodge polarization, arithmetic height drift, and spectral positivity. They are not four metaphors pasted together. They are four proposed incarnations of \(\Pi\), \(\mathsf{Diss}\), and \(\mathsf{Flux}\) on the same resolution-wake mechanism.

## Causal resolution holonomy

Two defects may be exactified in different orders:

\[
\Gamma_{z_2}\Gamma_{z_1}(S)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\Gamma_{z_1}\Gamma_{z_2}(S).
\]

Even if the resulting visible finite faces are equivalent, their future continuation laws may differ.

The comparison is the **causal resolution holonomy**

\[
\operatorname{Hol}_S(z_1,z_2)
=
\Gamma_{z_2}\Gamma_{z_1}
\Gamma_{z_2}^{-1}\Gamma_{z_1}^{-1}.
\]

The inverses here are comparison morphisms in a presentation groupoid, not executable reversal of time.

Exact genealogy need not be a coordinate-free fossil inside the endpoint. It can be an equivariant section over the torsor of resolution orders. The past survives as a twist in possible futures.

The wake therefore has no required reverse address. To obtain information caused by the past, one asks a future question and observes its holonomy.

## Generated path geometry

Two germs are close if no cheaply generated future question separates them:

\[
\tau(x,y)
=
\inf
\left\{
\Pi(q):
q\text{ is legally generated and }q(x)\neq q(y)
\right\},
\]

\[
d_{\mathfrak W}(x,y)=e^{-\tau(x,y)}.
\]

The metric is generated with the observables. It is not a metric on a set that existed first.

An “irrational” path is not a path with irrational coordinates. It is a locally computable continuation whose future distinction geometry never becomes finitely generated:

\[
\forall h\ \exists P_h
\text{ exact through horizon }h,
\qquad
\nexists P
\text{ exact for every legal future}.
\]

The completed wake is not the colimit of a fixed diagram because the indexing category of refinements is also born:

\[
\widehat{\mathfrak W}
\;\dreamsim\;
\varinjlim_{
q\in\mathcal Q(\widehat{\mathfrak W})
}
S_q.
\]

Querying the completion changes the diagram being completed.

## Polarization

The central fantasy requires more than exactification. It requires a positive or signed control law:

\[
\Pi_{\Gamma(S)}(\Gamma_*x)
-\Pi_S(x)
=
-\mathsf{Diss}_S(x)
+\mathsf{Flux}_S(x)
+\left\langle
\Omega_S,\,
\mathsf K_Sx
\right\rangle.
\]

Here:

- \(\mathsf{Diss}_S(x)\ge 0\) is defect dissipation;
- \(\mathsf{Flux}_S(x)\) is boundary flux through the current horizon;
- \(\Omega_S\) is genesis–Stokes curvature;
- \(\mathsf K_S\) couples curvature to transported primitives.

The dreamed master theorem is the **Polarized Wake Principle**:

> In a causally generated, functorial resolution-wake with coercive polarization, finite total boundary flux, exact finite-horizon continuation, and no nontrivial zero-cost causal holonomy, every finite-action phantom wake is globally exact through a bounded equivariant primitive.

This statement is almost certainly false as written. It is nevertheless the single imaginary engine for all four frontier routes below.

Its fantasy proof shape is:

1. Assume a minimal phantom wake.
2. Transport its finite primitives around all refinement loops.
3. Minimality makes the resulting holonomy parallel.
4. Coercivity says nontrivial parallel holonomy has positive action.
5. Universal exactification says the same holonomy bounds a generated primitive cell.
6. The resolution–Stokes law says its action is pure boundary flux.
7. Finite total flux drives the action to zero along the completion.
8. Positive and zero action collide, so the phantom cannot exist.

Every arrow in that argument may hide an abyss.

## Imagined route to three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes regularity

Take a Leray-style weak velocity field \(u\) as the root germ, but do not treat the continuum velocity field as the completed semantic object.

Let the finite question complex consist of:

- divergence-free spacetime test fields;
- localized kinetic-energy balances;
- pressure reconstruction tests;
- vorticity-flux and enstrophy-transfer tests on finite cylinders;
- compatibility questions across overlapping scales.

Local weak solvability supplies finite primitive groupoids. Pressure, vector potentials, localized flux decompositions, and gauge changes play the role of primitives.

The defect profile measures the least polarized cost of coherently assembling these local primitives across finer spacetime cylinders:

\[
\delta_{\mathrm{NS}}(u,h)
=
\inf
\left\{
\Pi_{\mathrm{NS}}(p):
p\text{ realizes all balance laws through scale }h
\right\}.
\]

Choose the polarization as a hybrid of kinetic energy, viscous dissipation, and a scale-sensitive vorticity action:

\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{NS}}
\;\dreamsim\;
\|u\|_{L^2}^2
+\nu\int|\nabla u|^2
+\text{cost of transporting vorticity primitives}.
\]

The genesis–Stokes defect is dreamed to isolate vortex stretching:

\[
\Omega_{\mathrm{NS}}
\;\dreamsim\;
(\omega\cdot\nabla)u,
\qquad
\omega=\nabla\times u.
\]

This would not say vortex stretching is literally a categorical commutator. It fantasizes that the part of exactification that fails to commute with scale-boundary refinement is precisely the nonlinear transfer capable of concentrating vorticity.

A finite-time singularity would be reinterpreted as a finite-action phantom wake:

- every finite family of weak tests is realizable;
- every finite scale admits local primitives;
- the completed datum exists weakly;
- but there is no bounded coherent primitive assembling smoothness through the singular time.

The Polarized Wake Principle would then prohibit blow-up if viscosity supplies genuine coercive dissipation and if vortex-stretching curvature cannot sustain zero-cost causal holonomy.

The imagined decisive inequality would not directly bound \(\|\omega\|_\infty\). It would say

\[
\operatorname{Hol}_{\mathrm{NS}}\neq 1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pi_{\mathrm{NS}}(\operatorname{Hol}_{\mathrm{NS}})
\le
\text{strictly dissipated wake flux},
\]

so a persistent concentration loop must spend positive resolution action faster than the finite energy budget allows.

If this route worked, smoothness would follow because a singularity could not remain in physical derivative degree; exactification would force its unresolved content into higher syzygy degree, where it no longer represents unbounded velocity derivatives.

That last sentence is gorgeous and extremely suspect.

### Missing Navier–Stokes bridges

- Kinetic energy in three dimensions is not coercive enough to control the required derivatives.
- Vortex stretching has no known sign making it a positive wake curvature.
- A weak solution might generate incompatible primitive towers.
- A blow-up might have infinite resolution action even while physical energy stays finite, evading the dreamed principle.
- “Migration into higher syzygy degree” does not imply analytic regularity.
- Constructing the exact functor from weak PDE data to the resolution-wake may already contain the regularity theorem.

## Imagined route to the Hodge conjecture

Let \(X\) be a smooth projective complex variety and let

\[
\alpha
\in
H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)
\cap
H^{p,p}(X).
\]

The root questions ask whether finite restrictions of \(\alpha\), after allowed refinements, can be represented by algebraic incidence data.

The primitive groupoid is not simply “all local algebraic cycles,” because that would smuggle in the conclusion. Dream instead of a hierarchy:

- local algebraic correspondences on finite covers;
- rational equivalences between their overlaps;
- higher discrepancies of those equivalences;
- compatible pushforwards through generated refinements;
- the torsor of all ways these data could assemble into a cycle class.

The wake attachment adds the entire incidence nerve of these local primitive candidates:

\[
\mathsf{Wake}_X(\alpha)
=
N\mathscr P_X(\alpha).
\]

The genesis–Stokes defect is the failure of the incidence data to glue:

\[
\Omega_X(\alpha)
=
d_{\Gamma X}j(\alpha)-j(d_X\alpha).
\]

Since \(\alpha\) is closed, this becomes pure generated gluing curvature.

Use the Hodge–Riemann polarization as \(\Pi_X\). The fantasy says that type \((p,p)\), rationality, and polarization together force the gluing holonomy to be unitary and positive:

\[
\Pi_X\!\left(
\operatorname{Hol}_X(\alpha)
\right)
\ge 0.
\]

The Hodge conjecture would become:

\[
\text{no polarized phantom wake exists for rational }(p,p)\text{ data}.
\]

If the Polarized Wake Principle killed the phantom, the universal equivariant primitive in the incidence wake would descend through its torsor of splittings. Its pushforward to \(X\) would be the required rational algebraic cycle.

The route’s imagined novelty is not “approximate cycles converge to a cycle.” It is:

> The algebraic cycle is the bounded global section of the full generated primitive geometry; its degree need not be uniformly guessed beforehand because the refinement wake generates exactly the incidence language needed to express it.

The degree could grow without bound through finite horizons yet the equivariant primitive might remain bounded in polarization. Thus polynomial degree would not be the fundamental cost. Causal holonomy would be.

### Missing Hodge bridges

- A rational Hodge class need not have the assumed finite local algebraic primitive data.
- Type \((p,p)\) does not obviously imply flat or unitary resolution holonomy.
- Hodge–Riemann positivity concerns primitive cohomology, not this invented incidence nerve.
- A bounded section of the invented wake need not push down to an honest algebraic cycle.
- Allowing generated refinements and correspondences may trivialize the problem by changing what counts as algebraic.
- The construction could merely restate the Hodge conjecture as the assertion that its primitive groupoid is nonempty.

The most beautiful likely lie here is that Hodge type is exactly the zero-curvature condition for algebraic genesis.

## Imagined route to Collatz

Let the root germ be the finite transition system generated by the accelerated Collatz operation on the residue information currently required to distinguish its branches.

Do not preselect moduli \(2^n\) using an external stage. Given the current unresolved orbit classes, generate the coarsest residue refinement on which the next descent question becomes meaningful.

Current questions include:

- whether an orbit segment descends below its starting height;
- which residue classes share a future tail;
- whether a finite branch admits a descent certificate;
- whether two descent certificates are compatible after collision.

Finite orbit segments are exact primitives. Collisions of segments are syzygies. The universal refinement wake exactifies every currently visible descent question by attaching the entire graph of its possible finite certificates.

For an orbit path \(\gamma\), dream a height holonomy

\[
\ell(\gamma)
=
\#\operatorname{odd}(\gamma)\log 3
-\#\operatorname{halving}(\gamma)\log 2
+\varepsilon(\gamma),
\]

where \(\varepsilon\) carries the accumulated distortion of the \(+1\) term.

The polarization is the least positive action required to transport a descent primitive around a residue cycle:

\[
\Pi_{\mathrm C}(\gamma)
=
\exp(\ell(\gamma))
+\text{collision-syzygy cost}.
\]

The \(1\to4\to2\to1\) cycle becomes the designated zero object or absorbing boundary component.

A divergent positive-integer orbit would produce an infinite legal path whose every finite prefix is realizable but whose descent primitive has no bounded global section. It would be a phantom wake.

The dreamed arithmetic assertion is that every nontrivial causal holonomy class has strictly inward mean flux:

\[
\operatorname{Hol}_{\mathrm C}(\gamma)\neq 1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\liminf_{h\to\infty}
\frac{1}{h}
\mathsf{Flux}_{\mathrm C}(\gamma|_h)
<0.
\]

Then the Polarized Wake Principle would force the only bounded recurrent holonomy to be the known terminal cycle. Every positive integer would eventually descend.

Perhaps the syzygies among colliding orbits create the missing global invariant. Instead of seeking a monotone scalar on individual integers, one seeks a monotone polarization on the entire groupoid of descent certificates. Individual paths may rise, while the geometry of alternative paths and collisions strictly contracts.

### Missing Collatz bridges

- The \(+1\) term prevents the logarithmic height from being a true cocycle.
- A divergent orbit might never create a finite detectable holonomy loop.
- Negative average drift on a statistical or residue level does not control every integer orbit.
- The generated residue refinement may simply encode the orbit step by step, hiding a stage counter inside modulus size.
- Proving strictly inward flux for every nontrivial holonomy may be exactly Collatz in different notation.
- A nonperiodic divergent path could live in the completion without any bounded finite witness, which is precisely the phenomenon the wake permits rather than excludes.

The beautiful lie is that orbit collisions supply enough extra geometry to turn nonuniform scalar drift into a coercive global contraction.

## Imagined route to the Riemann hypothesis

Let the root germ consist of finite prime data together with the finite explicit-formula questions currently meaningful.

Questions are compactly supported test functions, finite Euler-product identities, and finite comparisons between prime-side and zero-side observables.

Primitives are coherent logarithms and factorizations of truncated Euler products, together with all gauge changes among branch choices. Syzygies are multiplicative relations among prime primitives. The wake is the universal exactifier of the discrepancy between prime events and spectral events.

The dreamed resolution-wake carries a holonomy operator

\[
U_{\mathfrak W}
\]

acting on the completion of its primitive transports. The polarization comes from an explicit-formula quadratic form. From it one hallucinates a wake Laplacian

\[
\Delta_{\mathfrak W}
=
D_{\mathfrak W}^{*}D_{\mathfrak W}
+D_{\mathfrak W}D_{\mathfrak W}^{*}.

The outrageous bridge is a generated determinant identity:

\[
\det_{\mathrm{wake}}

\tfrac12+i\Delta_{\mathfrak W}^{1/2}-s
\right)
\;\dreamsim\;

\]

The functional equation

\[
\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)
\]

would be path reversal in the gauge groupoid of prime primitives. It would not reverse genesis itself; it would exchange the two boundary presentations of the same completed wake.

If \(\Delta_{\mathfrak W}\) were genuinely self-adjoint and nonnegative, its spectral parameters would be real:

\[
\rho
=
\tfrac12+i\lambda,
\qquad
\lambda\in\mathbb R.

Every nontrivial zero would lie on the critical line.

An off-line zero would instead be a finite-action phantom holonomy: invisible to every finite exact prime identity, yet surviving as nonunitary transport in the completed wake.

The Polarized Wake Principle would rule it out if the explicit-formula polarization were coercive on all unresolved prime syzygies.

The dream’s geometric slogan is:

> The critical line is the zero-flux surface where forward prime exactification and reverse spectral exactification have equal causal action.

### Missing Riemann bridges

- No canonical Hilbert space or self-adjoint wake operator has been constructed.
- The determinant identity is pure wish projection.
- Functional-equation symmetry alone does not imply unitarity or the Riemann hypothesis.
- Positivity of the needed explicit-formula form may already be equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis.
- Truncated Euler products do not converge in the critical strip in the naive required sense.
- Prime-factorization syzygies may be too trivial or too rigid to generate the zero spectrum.
- An off-line zero need not correspond to the invented bounded-assembly defect.

The most beautiful lie is the determinant formula. It is also the one most likely to be secretly restating the desired result.

## Cross-predictions of the single structure

If the Polarized Resolution-Wake were real, the four problems would not be the same theorem. They would share a failure mode.

### The common forbidden object

A counterexample in each domain would generate a class \(z\) such that

\[
\forall h,\qquad
\mathscr P(z;h)\neq\varnothing,
\]

but

\[
\nexists
\text{ bounded equivariant section }
p_\infty
\in
\varprojlim_h\mathscr P(z;h).
\]

- Navier–Stokes: a weakly realizable but non-smooth singular flow.
- Hodge: a rational \((p,p)\) class with no global algebraic primitive.
- Collatz: an infinite positive orbit with no global descent primitive.
- Riemann: a prime–spectral discrepancy producing nonunitary completed holonomy.

All four would be **polarized phantom wakes**.

### The four polarizations would predict each other’s missing lemma

- Navier–Stokes would ask whether dissipation is strong enough to polarize resolution holonomy.
- Hodge would ask whether intersection positivity controls generated incidence holonomy.
- Collatz would ask whether arithmetic height plus collision geometry produces a coercive form.
- Riemann would ask whether explicit-formula positivity is the spectral polarization of prime syzygies.

The cross-prediction is that the missing estimate in each problem should not be a direct pointwise bound. It should be a positivity theorem on a groupoid of alternative finite primitives.

- the scaling-critical channel of three-dimensional fluid concentration;
- the balanced \((p,p)\) bidegree;

This may be poetry wearing an equation.

The dream therefore expects probability to become useful after the exactification and holonomy structure is built, not before.

## A mechanically executable toy skeleton

The local continuation law, stripped of domain content, would be:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak D &\leftarrow
\text{the full finite groupoid of admitted unresolved certificates in }S,\\
\text{if }\mathfrak D&\simeq 0,\quad\text{return }S,\\
\mathscr P&\leftarrow
\text{the full primitive groupoid of }\mathfrak D,\\
W&\leftarrow N\mathscr P,\\
S^+&\leftarrow S\cup_{\operatorname{ev}}W,\\
\Omega&\leftarrow d_{S^+}j-jd_S,\\
\mathcal Q^+&\leftarrow\operatorname{Syz}(\mathscr P)\oplus\Omega,\\
\Pi^+&\leftarrow
\text{transport }\Pi\text{ through the resolution–Stokes law},\\
\text{return }S^+&.
\end{aligned}
\]

There is no stage argument. The current semantic defect causes the attachment.

Nevertheless, the size of \(S\) may reveal the number of iterations. That does not invalidate causal continuation, but it means the construction is not “counter-free” in the magical sense. The dream’s invariant is not ignorance of age. It is that age alone cannot cause birth.

For local computability, the groupoids \(\mathfrak D\) and \(\mathscr P\) must be finitely materializable from a finite state. For global non-finite isolation, their recursively generated future distinction geometry must escape every fixed finite presentation.

Those requirements may conflict.

## Suspected contradictions

1. **The full-defect attachment may be infinite.** Avoiding a selector by attaching every symmetric defect can destroy finite local computability immediately.

2. **The generated site is circular.** Questions determine the next state, but the next state determines which questions were semantically legal. There may be no fixed point or coinductive solution.

3. **Universal exactification may trivialize everything.** If every obstruction is killed by freely adjoining a primitive, the wake may become a contractible garbage collector rather than a discriminating theory.

4. **Syzygies may not preserve semantic content.** “Unresolvedness migrates in degree” is an attractive slogan, not a theorem. Exactification may lose exactly the information we hoped to conserve.

5. **Polarization may fail to transport.** An energy or intersection form on the old complex may have no natural extension over freely generated primitive cells.

6. **Positive action need not imply bounded assembly.** Local positivity without compactness, completeness, or a uniform estimate does not rule out escape to infinity.

7. **No zero-cost holonomy may be equivalent to the target theorem.** The master axiom could conceal every hard part.

8. **The wake completion may not exist.** The diagram changes as it is completed, and legal queries may cause mutually incompatible refinements.

9. **History and semantic descent may conflict.** If exact genealogy is torsorial rather than intrinsic, the holonomy needed to remember it may obstruct the claim that continuation depends only on present semantic state.

10. **The four examples use incompatible meanings of exactness.** PDE weak exactness, algebraic-cycle gluing, arithmetic orbit certification, and spectral explicit formulas may not inhabit any honest common category.

11. **Completion without bounded assembly cuts both ways.** It supplies the language for phantom wakes but gives no reason they cannot exist. The foundational object may organize counterexamples rather than eliminate them.

12. **This may be renamed obstruction theory.** The dream could collapse into familiar local-to-global machinery with new theatrical vocabulary and no new theorem.
