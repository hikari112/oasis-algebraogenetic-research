# Conjecture uniqueness audit and revised frontier

## Status

- **Cards frozen:** 2026-08-10
- **Audit completed:** 2026-08-11

This is a bounded literature and theorem-shape audit of the six conjecture
rungs in
[the marked Fox--Hodge bridge](./endogenous-conjecture-ladder-and-fox-hodge-bridge.md),
the four native mathematical targets, the AI target, and the
[CCIL protocol](./counterfactual-constraint-inversion-lab.md).

The conclusion is deliberately asymmetric:

- the six rungs are not six equally novel conjectures;
- several components and two whole theorem *forms* are established prior art;
- one rung is missing hypotheses and one is not yet a fully frozen theorem;
- no inspected source states the strongest remaining causal-extraction plus
  architectural-separation conjunction; and
- that bounded no-match result is not a proof of priority or uniqueness.

The correct public claim is therefore **apparently unmatched conjunction in
this corpus**, never “no prior art” or “unique theorem.”

## 3. Rung I: repair before conjecturing

Coherence for pseudonatural transformations, rewriting-based coherence,
Beck--Chevalley transport, Karoubi completion, compiler DAGs, and equality
saturation already cover individual clauses. They do not imply one uniform
normalization theorem over the current heterogeneous class
\(\mathsf{AdmOld}\).

The present quantifier omits the conditions needed to push base changes,
decide or certify the relevant rewrite steps, compile all semantic zigzags,
and control cost. Finite syntax alone is insufficient: Kobayashi and Katsura
construct
[finitely presented monoids with no finite complete presentation](https://eudml.org/doc/135529),
and even restricted typed convertibility can have extreme complexity.

The honest replacement target is:

> For a preregistered class of finite higher-dimensional rewrite packages
> with a finite-derivation property, proof-carrying future equivalence,
> Beck--Chevalley-compatible base change, coherent idempotent splitting, and
> an additive DAG cost, prove a future-role-preserving certified normal form
> with a computable distortion \(f_{\Xi}(B)\).

An affine bound \(a_\Xi B+b_\Xi\) should be a later strengthening, not part of
the first conjecture.

Close sources include Lucas on
[pseudonatural coherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.07807), Mimram on
[rewriting-based coherence](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSCD.2023.16),
Nguyễn on
[TOWER-complete typed convertibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12601), and
Willsey et al. on [equality saturation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03082).

## 4. Rung II: a classical calibration exists

Let

\[
X_m=\langle a,b\mid[a,b]^m\rangle,
\qquad m\geq2.
\]

Every \(X_m\) has the same rooted one-skeleton, the same zero ordinary
two-boundary, the same integral homology \((\mathbf Z,\mathbf Z^2,\mathbf Z)\),
and the same abelianized attaching word. Fox differentiation retains the
factor

\[
1+[a,b]+\cdots+[a,b]^{m-1},
\]

while the one-relator torsion theorem makes the maximal finite order in
\(\pi_1(X_m)\) exactly \(m\). Thus the paired family
\((X_{2n},X_{2n+1})\) already supplies growing lower-shadow collisions with a
single preregistrable separator.

The exact family was publicly discussed in a 2025
[MathOverflow question](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/500488/a-question-regarding-a-family-of-one-relator-groups).
This does not settle the project-specific claim: the actual observer
\(\operatorname{Sh}_{\leq1}\), future-role functor \(U\), and genetic/future
quotients still have to be frozen and checked. If the observer already exposes
torsion or the relevant Fox factor, the candidate fails. Otherwise Rung II
becomes a theorem-backed calibration, not a frontier novelty claim.

## 5. Rung III: the strongest surviving mathematical conjecture

CEGAR, CEGIS, finite tell-tales, approximation profiles, local permutation
stability, and proof search each collide with one part of Rung III. No
inspected source supplies their conjunction with:

1. only proof-carrying local word-equality or word-nontriviality queries;
2. no fatal portfolio, stage counter, or list of future challenges at birth;
3. presentation, gauge, stutter, and support-thinning equivariance;
4. stutter on the same unresolved semantic state;
5. a fairness law published independently of success and closed under finite
   delays; and
6. finite termination on every such run at one explicit finite obstruction
   for the fixed nonsofic group.

The primary nonsofic proof is qualitative at the required interface. Chapter
3 of [*Ten Advances in Mathematics and Theoretical Computer
Science*](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/ten-proofs-oai.pdf) uses asymptotic
\(o(N)\) estimates, expansion, matching, repair, and sufficiently large
indices to force Thompson's \(V\) to be LEF. It does not print a numerical
finite \((F,\varepsilon)\) threshold.

The surviving target has two ordered layers.

### III-Q: quantitative extraction

Make the expansion, matching, component-repair, and word-error losses explicit
and derive one numerical finite \((F,\varepsilon)\) obstruction for the fixed
group. This is a proof-mining/effectivization problem, not yet algebraogenesis.

### III-C: causal extraction

On the resulting exact target, construct a history-derived well-founded rank
on semantic failure states and a local selector whose non-stuttering steps
strictly decrease the rank. Prove that every frozen-fair run reaches the
obstruction without the target portfolio being supplied as advice.

The order matters. Attempting III-C before III-Q lets qualitative compactness
or a hidden fatal set masquerade as termination.

Closest sources include Clarke et al.'s
[CEGAR](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~emc/papers/Papers%20In%20Refereed%20Journals/Counterexample-guided%20abstraction%20refinement.pdf),
Angluin's [finite tell-tale framework](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0019-9958(80)90285-5),
Arzhantseva--Cherix
[metric-approximation profiles](https://doi.org/10.5802/afst.1788), and
Bradford's [local permutation stability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.15249).
They establish neighboring machinery, not the frozen conjunction.

## 6. Rung IV: split support from phase

For a unitary \(U\in U(N)\) and rank-one diagonal atoms, define

\[
\delta_{\rm at}(U)
=\frac1N\sum_i\left(1-\sum_j|U_{ji}|^4\right).
\]

The matrix \(P_{ji}=|U_{ji}|^2\) is bistochastic. Birkhoff--von Neumann
decomposition and phase matching give a monomial unitary \(M\) satisfying,
for normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm,

\[
\|U-M\|_{2,N}\leq\sqrt{2\delta_{\rm at}(U)}.
\]

This explicit one-operator **support** result is elementary infrastructure,
not a new Fox--Hodge theorem. It does not cover approximate unitarity,
simultaneous generator consistency, or any Fox/role observable.

The real gap is simultaneous:

\[
\text{approximate atomic normalizers with marked data}
\longrightarrow
\text{one permutation model preserving relations, Fox data, and separation}.
\]

Exact monomial normalizers are permutations decorated by phase cocycles.
Conditional-expectation overlaps see their support, while raw matrices and
evaluated Fox operators can see the phase. The exact companion
[phase/gauge probe](./genesis-phase-gauge-rounding-probe.md) compares
\(U=+I\) and \(U=-I\) for \(C_2=\langle s\mid s^2\rangle\). They are exact
unitary diagonal-MASA normalizers with identical support transport and exact
relator satisfaction, but their raw generators and evaluated Fox derivatives
\(I+U\) differ. Diagonal conjugation cannot remove the central scalar phase.

This does not refute monomial-valued rounding or a theorem with a
predeclared phase quotient. It proves that Rung IV must freeze one of three
contracts:

- retain and control phase-sensitive Fox observables;
- add a dimension-independent phase-cocycle elimination theorem; or
- deliberately use a phase-blind observable and separately prove that it
  still carries the required nonsofic role.

The current symbols \(\delta_{\rm Fox}\), \(\delta_{\rm role}\), \(d_q\), and
\(\mathsf{LocHdg}_q\) specify roles rather than formulas. Until a natural,
target-free defect grammar is frozen, the full Rung IV is a conjecture schema,
not a collision-ready theorem.

Generic unitary stability cannot silently fill the gap. Eckhardt and Shulman
construct an
[amenable Hilbert--Schmidt-stable group that is not permutation
stable](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.01089). The additional atomic, phase, Fox,
and role structure must do genuine work.

## 7. Rung V: all slogans are old; the coupled lower bound was not found

Online communication and cell-probe lower bounds already formalize piecewise
revelation and answer-before-next-input causality. Adaptive-adversary work
already lets future operations depend on prior outputs. Adaptive indexing,
materialized-view selection, and Adapton already grow representations or
dependency graphs in response to workload and demand.

These sources kill any claim that “the representation adapts when a new
question arrives” is novel. They do not state the frozen Rung-V conjunction:

- the same causal transcript for both architectures;
- every source, state, address, compiler, proof, precision, and replay cost
  charged;
- a positively closed old doctrine rather than one restricted data-structure
  model;
- a total quantitative reduction to an independently certified obstruction
  margin;
- finite old realizability at every individual stage; and
- a divergent relative ratio
  \(m_{\rm old}(n)/c_{\rm adm}(n)\to\infty\).

No direct frozen-card match was found. The correct classification is a
project-specific conjectural synthesis. Its next hostile control is one old
baseline combining online communication, adaptive-adversary robustness,
workload-generated views/indexes, and demand-driven dependency graphs under
the exact same cost ledger.

Primary close sources include
[online communication cell-probe bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.06185),
[adaptive-adversary dynamic algorithms](https://research.google/pubs/dynamic-algorithms-against-an-adaptive-adversary-generic-constructions-and-lower-bounds/),
[adaptive indexing](https://doi.org/10.1145/2247596.2247667), and
[Adapton](http://www.cs.umd.edu/~mwh/papers/hammer13adaptontr.html).

## 8. Rung VI: the schema has a name

The form “no conservative natural translation realizes this family” belongs
to established work on institutions, morphisms/comorphisms, relative
expressiveness, interpretability, and Morita equivalence. Rung VI is not a new
theorem schema. Generic signature universes and extensible compilers instead
act as constructive collapse candidates for any proposed witness.

Any original content must be one explicit tuple

\[
(\Xi_0,\mathsf H^*,U,\Omega)
\]

whose invariant \(\Omega\) is defined independently, preserved by every
positively listed old constructor, violated by the admitted family, and robust
against extensible compilers, higher-inductive or inductive-recursive
universes, completion, pro-objects, and generic interpreters.

The natural formal language is institution theory. Goguen and Roşu's
[institution morphisms](https://doi.org/10.1007/s001650200013), Fernandes on
[translational expressiveness](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08481),
[Pyrosome](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06360), and
[higher-inductive-inductive signature theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.00297)
are direct baselines and kill tests.

## 9. The native targets are real, but mostly already named

The audit does not remove the big problems from scope. It types our ambition
more accurately.

| Target | Existing open core | Project-specific extra burden |
|---|---|---|
| Hodge | Producing a rational codimension-\(p\) algebraic cycle for every class in \(H^{2p}(X,\mathbf Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X,\mathbf C)\) on a smooth projective complex variety is exactly the rational Hodge Conjecture. | Effective bounds, a computable construction, functorial choice, and endogenous admission are stronger. |
| 3D Navier--Stokes | A universal a priori critical bound coupled to a continuation criterion would prove global regularity. Critical continuation criteria already exist; no such full-class universal bound is known. | One preregistered causal generation law and quantitative coercive estimate. |
| Collatz | An unrestricted well-founded descending rank is equivalent to termination, since the hitting time supplies such a rank if Collatz is true. | Synthesis inside a frozen local language without stopping-time or truth advice. |
| RH | Self-adjoint spectral realization is the Hilbert--Pólya program; the Weil criterion and Connes' partial or semilocal trace-formula framework are established neighboring programs. | Construct the operator or Hermitian form from arithmetic input with an RH-independent domain and exact correspondence. |
| AI | Universal approximation and adaptive computation are established. | External universality plus a uniform fully charged lower bound against a frozen old observer class under identical causal information. |

Authoritative anchors are the Clay pages for the
[Hodge Conjecture](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/),
[Navier--Stokes](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/navier-stokes-equation/),
and [RH](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/), together
with Tao's current
[almost-all Collatz theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03562). None supplies
the project-specific admission mechanism.

## 10. CCIL and the discovery-method names

No inspected source supplied all seven frozen CCIL roles. The protocol is best
described provisionally as a controlled synthesis of:

\[
\text{design fiction/backcasting}
+\text{abduction/reverse mathematics/weakest preconditions}
+\text{CEGIS/CEGAR}
+\text{finite compilation and verification}
+\text{blinded method comparison}.
\]

The exact name had no same-method hit in the bounded corpus, but that is not a
priority proof. “Conceptual cartography” is existing philosophy terminology,
so the project should say **CCIL destination map** or **project-specific
conceptual-cartography phase**. The meaningful object is the extensional
seven-condition protocol, not ownership of ordinary words.

Backcasting, [research through design
fiction](https://doi.org/10.1145/2556288.2557098), reverse mathematics,
[CEGAR](https://doi.org/10.1145/876638.876643), automated theory formation,
and a [blinded LLM ideation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04109) are close
components. Whether their CCIL synthesis is more productive is an empirical
hypothesis for the preregistered 160-trial experiment.

## 11. What may actually be new

After removing the classical output problems, elementary rounding, adaptive
materialization, and standard noninterpretability language, one candidate
remains:

> **Surviving core conjecture.** For the fixed theorem-backed nonsofic proof
> configuration, a local-query, presentation-equivariant, stutter-invariant
> causal process can extract one explicit finite obstruction on every
> independently fair run, without a clock or preloaded fatal portfolio; the
> resulting admitted representation achieves the same future role with
> asymptotically smaller fully charged cost than every construction in one
> preregistered composite old doctrine.

No inspected source presented this whole conjunction as a single conjecture.
It joins an effective nonsofic witness theorem, a counter-free causal
selection theorem, and a relative architectural lower bound. Every part has
strong neighbors; the conjunction is apparently unmatched in this bounded
audit.

That is the strongest bounded originality candidate left by this audit. It is
also much harder than coining a new object: both the finite obstruction and
the lower-bound comparator must be constructed explicitly.

## 12. Direct path forward

1. **Extract the finite nonsofic constants.** Replace every \(o(N)\),
   “sufficiently large,” matching loss, and repair tolerance in the proof by
   explicit moduli, and compile one numerical finite obstruction.
2. **Freeze Rung IV's formulas.** Decide whether the downstream observable is
   raw, phase-aware, or intentionally phase-blind; use the phase/gauge probe as
   a mandatory unit test.
3. **Close the Rung-II calibration.** Instantiate the exact old observer and
   future-role quotient on \(X_m=\langle a,b\mid[a,b]^m\rangle\).
4. **Repair Rung I.** Define the restricted rewrite/coherence class and prove a
   computable distortion before asking for an affine one.
5. **Build Rung V's strongest old baseline.** Combine adaptive indexing,
   generated views, demanded computation, online access, and fair adaptive
   adversaries under the same ledger.
6. **Keep the big four native.** A Hodge bridge must output a cycle; an
   Navier--Stokes bridge a critical a priori estimate; a Collatz bridge a
   restricted-language descent certificate; and an RH bridge a self-adjoint
   operator or an equivalent positive Hermitian form.

The first item is the most direct theorem gap. It is simultaneously close to
the existing exact nonsofic spine, necessary for Rung III, and independent of
whether the later architectural interpretation succeeds.

## 13. Claim boundary

This audit supports:

- exact positive prior-art collisions listed above;
- the statement that no full match was found for Rung III, Rung V, or the
  CCIL protocol in the searched corpus; and
- a disciplined revision of the research order.

It does not support:

- global novelty or priority;
- proof of any surviving conjecture;
- a consequence for Hodge, Navier--Stokes, Collatz, RH, or AI; or
- the claim that one missing theorem must solve all five targets.

Those remain research ambitions. The purpose of the audit is to make them
attackable without shrinking them.
