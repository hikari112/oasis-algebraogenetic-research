The sweep was deliberately broad in mathematical field and narrow in structural scope. It asked one question:

> Which mature theories already implement some part of computable local continuation, path-sensitive semantics, obstruction-driven resolution, growing observables, or finite-to-complete realization—and what exact arrow remains missing?

## 1. Executive conclusion

The research is not pointing toward a new probability theory, a mysterious scalar continuum, or an ordinary algebra with exotic coefficients. It is pointing toward a process that changes the **available tests and representations** of a semantic object.

The strongest surviving formulation is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{retain the full homotopy type of each solution problem;}\\
\text{let its obstruction and monodromy generate a minimal new probe module;}\\
\text{realize that probe by actual continuation;}\\
\text{then recompute which questions have become meaningful.}
\end{gathered}}
\]

This is a course correction from “create a fresh state or transport axis.” The existing degree no-go is real: resolving an obstruction in cohomological degree \(m\) naturally produces higher \((m-1)\)-transport, not a new element of \(\pi_1\). The cleaner move is to refine the observable or representation algebra so that old paths, previously identical at their endpoints, become distinguishable.

Every major neighboring theory supplies a static part of this picture:

- stacks and derived moduli retain full solution groupoids;
- obstruction theory and algebraic weak factorization systems supply universal fillers for declared lifting problems;
- polygraphs and Squier completion turn critical branchings into higher cells;
- Toda brackets, Massey products, and homotopy algebras retain failed lower coherence as higher operations;
- inductive-recursive and higher inductive families can generate contexts, dependent families, and coherences together;
- holonomy, Tannaka reconstruction, and groupoid algebras turn transport into observables;
- rough-path signatures provide exact compositional path features and arbitrary finite truncation of one completed object;
- regularity structures generate equation-dependent symbolic grammars;
- domain theory and coalgebraic fixpoints present infinite behavior through finite information; and
- predictive-state, Hankel-rank, and Koopman methods refine state through future tests or observables.

The missing theorem is not one of those nouns. It is the feedback arrow:

\[
\boxed{
\text{native failed solution fiber}
\longrightarrow
\text{canonical new admissible probe}
\longrightarrow
\text{native realization}
\longrightarrow
\text{next solution problem}.
}
\]

No source in the sweep supplied that complete, counter-free, presentation-invariant feedback law. The project does not yet supply it either.

## 2. Reflection on the path

The project began by asking for an externally universal, internally non-sofic approximator. That phrasing encouraged several successive misidentifications:

1. perhaps the object was a probability distribution;
2. perhaps it was a continuously completed memory;
3. perhaps a high-degree obstruction directly emitted a new ordinary direction;
4. perhaps unbounded witness depth alone implied non-soficity; or
5. perhaps a completed infinite algebra was the process.

The exact finite work removed these possibilities one by one.

- Probability remained a state on an already declared observable algebra.
- Completion retained infinite semantic data but did not explain which distinction should be generated next.
- The Postnikov degree audit prohibited silently lowering a higher obstruction into first transport.
- The bar resolution showed that unending transparent genealogy can coexist with a finite semantic representer.
- The split, thin, and loop-gauge controls showed that extension class, coefficient policy, and path retention are separate causal inputs.

The strongest exact residue is the current eight-state \(D_8\) laboratory:

- the free coefficient carrier has an empty coherent-lift fiber;
- the selected \(\mathbb Z/4_\chi\) carrier has sixteen lifts, eight gauge components, and rank-one component holonomy;
- the thin carrier has a contractible singleton fiber; and
- concrete loops can move between lift components while the endpoint factor set and selected residue stay fixed.

That calculation suggests a more foundational trichotomy:

\[
\operatorname{Sol}(q)\simeq
\begin{cases}
\varnothing,
& \text{unresolved: attach a vertical repair};\\[2mm]
\text{inhabited but noncontractible},
& \text{resolved with choices: expose symmetry and monodromy};\\[2mm]
\ast,
& \text{contractibly resolved: stutter}.
\end{cases}
\]

A question is therefore not merely a proposition with answer yes or no. Its answer is a solution homotopy type. Genesis should respond to the full type rather than its propositional truncation.

## 4. The candidate object

### 4.1 The policy-indexed solution diagram

Let \(\mathcal C\) be a current semantic path category or \(\infty\)-category, and let \(\mathsf{Pol}\) be the current category of meaningful coefficient, relation, or resolution policies. Define

\[
\mathsf{Lift}:
\mathcal B^{\mathrm{op}}\times\mathsf{Pol}^{\mathrm{op}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal S,
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal B\) is the retained path or presentation groupoid;
- \(\mathcal S\) is the \(\infty\)-category of spaces; and
- \(\mathsf{Lift}(b,p)\) is the full solution homotopy type, not merely its set of components or its existence bit.

Its Grothendieck construction

\[
\int\mathsf{Lift}\longrightarrow \mathcal B\times\mathsf{Pol}
\]

retains changes of policy, gauge, path transport, and higher coherence in one object. This packaging is standard straightening/unstraightening or stack technology. Calling it a “resolution spectrum” does not make it novel.

What it buys is causal honesty. The project no longer needs to select one convenient \(\mathbb Z/4\) policy before seeing the rest of the diagram. It can ask:

- where does a lift first become possible?
- where do components or automorphisms appear?
- which monodromy actions persist across natural policy maps?
- where does the fiber become contractible?
- which features are invariant under the policy symmetry group?

### 4.2 Monodromy-born observable refinement

For a retained fiber and a base loop, ordinary monodromy gives representations such as

\[
\rho_{p,k}:
\pi_1(\mathcal B,b)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Aut}
\bigl(H_k(\mathsf{Lift}(b,p);K)\bigr),
\]

together with the permutation action on \(\pi_0\). Let \(\mathcal O\) be the current observable category or algebra. A first candidate extension is

\[
\mathcal O^+
=
\left\langle
\mathcal O,\,
\operatorname{Coeff}(\rho_{p,k})
\right\rangle_{\mathrm{declared\ closure}}.
\]

The strict-gain test is relative:

\[
\frac{\mathcal O^+}
{\langle\mathcal O\rangle_{\mathrm{declared\ closure}}}
\ne 0.
\]

This step does not create a new physical or group-theoretic direction. It creates a new callable distinction on the existing semantic carrier. That is exactly the interpretation suggested by:

> The learner may not be estimating hidden states. It may be growing the topology in which hidden states become distinguishable.

For the finite \(D_8\) fiber, the loop action translates the eight components along a rank-one line. Linearizing that action yields an anti-invariant observable module that detects a path effect erased by endpoint quotienting. The representation is standard; deriving its admission and semantic test is not yet done.

### 4.3 Goal-indexed growth of a restricted probe category

The deepest correction is that there may be no canonical goal-free growth rule. If every possible distinction is equally admissible, the “self-growing algebra” either appends everything or makes an arbitrary choice.

Let

\[
\mathcal P_n\hookrightarrow\mathcal C
\]

be the finite category of currently materialized probes. A fixed semantic object \(X\) is visible through its restricted Yoneda-like profile

\[
Y_{\mathcal P_n}(X)
=
\operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal C}(-,X)
\big|_{\mathcal P_n^{\mathrm{op}}}.
\]

A typed future test \(\tau\) identifies a distinction that the current profile cannot represent. The obstruction is not “the state is incomplete.” It is:

\[
\text{the current test category cannot make the requested future distinction descend.}
\]

Genesis should construct the least extension

\[
\mathcal P_n\hookrightarrow\mathcal P_{n+1}
\]

that represents the obstruction-born monodromy distinction and makes \(\tau\) computable. The full presheaf or Tannakian completion is then the **fossil** of all possible tests. The causal process is the finite, certificate-bearing path through probe-category extensions.

This formulation cleanly separates two branches:

- **epistemic genesis:** \(X\) stays fixed and new probes reveal existing distinctions;
- **ontic genesis:** the successor semantic object must change so that the new probe becomes executable.

The first is mathematically cheaper and should be tested first. The second needs a native realization theorem.

### 4.4 A working definition

An **algebraogenetic resolution doctrine** is provisionally a tuple

\[
\mathfrak G
=
(\mathcal C,\mathsf{Pol},\mathsf{Lift},\mathcal P,\mathsf{Real},\Gamma)
\]

with:

1. a path-sensitive semantic category \(\mathcal C\);
2. a generated policy category \(\mathsf{Pol}\);
3. a full policy-indexed solution-space diagram \(\mathsf{Lift}\);
4. a finite currently materialized probe category \(\mathcal P\);
5. a native realization relation \(\mathsf{Real}\); and
6. one invariant successor operator \(\Gamma\).

The operator \(\Gamma\) must satisfy:

1. semantic and presentation descent;
2. gauge and base-change naturality;
3. zero-obstruction and contractible-solution stutter;
4. no selected torsor origin or basis vector;
5. retention of precisely the paths used by the semantic test;
6. universal minimality of the admitted probe extension;
7. native executability of every new probe;
8. confluence for independent admissions;
9. local finiteness at every materialized stage;
10. no future table or external stage counter; and
11. a strict gain against a declared fixed-observer class.

This is a candidate interface, not a constructed new field.

## 5. The most direct theorem target

The next theorem should not mention Hodge, Navier–Stokes, Collatz, RH, or AI.

> **Resolution-monodromy admission target.**
>
> For a declared category of finite semantic problems satisfying explicit smallness and descent hypotheses, construct an initial path-retaining extension that admits the minimal probe module generated by solution-relevant monodromy of the full obstruction-selected lift diagram. Prove that it is presentation-invariant, stutters on contractibly solved fibers, is computable without a stage counter, and yields a strict cost or rank advantage over a declared bounded fixed-doctrine observer class.

This statement has two halves:

- the **foundational half** constructs the admission law; and
- the **architectural half** proves that it is not merely a verbose simulation of a fixed global dictionary.

Without the first, the process is hand-designed. Without the second, it is standard adaptive revelation inside a completed algebra.

## 6. Immediate finite program

### Target A — Complete the \(D_8\) policy-fiber diagram

Start from the universal relation-policy representer already constructed in the project.

1. Enumerate a categorically declared finite region of \(Q\)-stable relation subgroups \(L\subseteq A\), not an ordered list of favored carriers.
2. Construct the cochain-level lift groupoid for every \(B_L=M/L\).
3. Compute policy maps, automorphism actions, nerves, and \(\pi_0,\pi_1,\pi_2\) where finite.
4. Determine the upward-closed nullification locus and its minimal symmetry orbits.
5. Locate the free empty fiber, the noncontractible \(\mathbb Z/4_\chi\) fiber, and the thin contractible fiber inside the same diagram.
6. Test whether \(\mathbb Z/4_\chi\) is initial, minimal, one object in an invariant antichain, or an artifact of the old four-policy fence.

**Kill test:** if the entire diagram is only a fixed derived mapping stack and no invariant admission rule emerges, the packaging is standard and the proposed successor has not been built.

### Target B — Collision audit against established completion theories

Encode the exact OP/PO repair system:

- as a 2-polygraph and compute its first Squier or homotopical completion;
- as a finite lifting problem and compute the relevant algebraic weak-factorization repair;
- as a monodromy representation and its groupoid/Tannakian closures; and
- as one fixed inductive-recursive or higher-inductive signature.

**Pass condition:** the semantic admission rule produces a presentation-invariant callable probe or native continuation absent from all four comparisons without an extra rule.

**Kill condition:** one standard completion carries the entire successor, recurrence, and path semantics. Then the proposed mechanism is an instance of that theory.

### Target C — One genuine feedback step

From the complete diagram:

1. extract the least solution-relevant monodromy subrepresentation outside the old closure;
2. adjoin its full module, not a selected coordinate;
3. realize it by continuation;
4. recompute the policy-fiber diagram; and
5. demonstrate a new typed question that was not callable before.

The step must be unchanged under basis changes, policy equivalences, torsor-origin shifts, and certificate presentation.

### Target D — Restricted nonrationality and complexity

Define a natural bounded global observer class that already includes the strongest obvious controls:

- bounded program features;
- bounded-dimensional matrix coefficients;
- bounded polygraphic depth;
- fixed signature or Koopman dictionaries; and
- finite rational or Hankel-rank representations.

Then prove an explicit family in which:

\[
\text{observer rank/cost must diverge}
\qquad\text{while}\qquad
\text{local generated probes remain cheaper}.
\]
Matched finite, sofic, and theorem-backed non-sofic controls are mandatory. Infinite depth, nonclosed range, nontrivial monodromy, or unbounded signature degree alone do not imply non-soficity.

### Target E — Native toy realization

Before classical three-dimensional Navier–Stokes, use a model with a known regular/blow-up or solvable/obstructed dichotomy. Two strong controls are:

- a rough or singular equation with a known fixed signature/regularity grammar; and
- a finite filtered complex with an exact Hodge decomposition.

Compare a fixed grammar with an obstruction-adaptive grammar. Prove local finiteness, confluence, reconstruction, and schedule-independent certification.

## 7. What probability, optimization, memory, polynomials, and fractals become

### Probability

A probability distribution is a positive normalized functional on the observables that currently exist:

\[
\omega_n:\mathcal O_n\to\mathbb C.
\]

It can guide uncertainty, prediction, or probe priority. It does not define the carrier unless a theorem shows that the probabilistic state universally forces the next admissible probe.

### Optimization

Optimization chooses a representative, repair, or readout after the legal problem and observable types exist. A loss-maximizing feature from a hidden universal dictionary is not genesis. A canonical minimum-residual representative may become part of the theory only after the solution diagram and its semantic quotient are fixed.

### Memory

The foundational memory is neither a reversible tape nor an address. It is exact compositional transport:

- the inclusion history of probe categories;
- the holonomy action on solution fibers;
- the Chen-like composition law for paths; and
- the certificate explaining why each probe became legal.

The full replay log is optional audit data. The exact compositional invariant is the mathematical core.

### Polynomials

The Dickson classes and related polynomials are exact obstruction detectors and coordinate shadows of the current finite geometry. They are not the generation law. Higher-dimensional rewriting and derived geometry suggest that polynomial identities should be read as boundaries, relations, or characteristic classes inside a larger resolution process.

### Fractals and completion

Cantor, profinite, domain-theoretic, and rough-path completions explain how every finite view can be computable while the semantic object is not finitely isolated or rational. The “irrationality” is structural:

\[
\text{the behavior lies beyond every finite/rational presentation}
\]

rather than involving transcendental numerical coefficients. A branching or fractal completion is a plausible fossil of the process, not by itself its cause.

## 8. External universality and internal non-soficity

The desired conjunction is coherent but still unproved.

Let

\[
\mathcal O_\infty
=
\overline{\bigcup_n\mathcal O_n}.
\]

External universality could mean that \(\bigcup_n\mathcal O_n\) separates the relevant semantic points and is dense in a target function algebra. Path signatures, matrix coefficients, and Stone–Weierstrass-type arguments provide established templates.

Internal non-soficity must be stated separately. The exact transport action, or a quotient retaining every generated distinction, must carry a proved finite \((F,\varepsilon)\) obstruction to sofic approximation. The [OpenAI non-sofic construction](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/ten-proofs-oai.pdf) supplies the motivating exact group-theoretic source, not the admission theorem.

The architecture-level goal is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{finite readouts approximate external tasks on }\mathcal O_\infty,\\
&\text{the exact generator/transport engine is never replaced by a finite emulator,}\\
&\text{and no bounded fixed observer preserves every born distinction.}
\end{aligned}}
\]

This captures “we do not approximate the approximator.” The exact process generates the probe algebra; approximation happens only in downstream task readouts.

## 9. Implications for the named frontiers

### Hodge

The [rational Hodge conjecture remains open](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/). Recent counterexamples to integral variants must not be conflated with it. A real bridge requires:

1. a family of cycle-realization homotopy types;
2. a precise policy base and monodromy action;
3. compatibility with rational Hodge classes and the cycle-class map;
4. a polarization or Hodge filtration on the generated complex; and
5. a theorem that the generated probe is algebraically realized.

Nonabelian Hodge theory, Gauss–Manin transport, wild/Stokes data, and vanishing cycles validate the representation/monodromy language. They do not supply the missing cycle-realization theorem.

### Navier–Stokes

The [classical three-dimensional existence-and-smoothness problem remains open](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/navier-stokes-equation/). The serious route is not “finite local solutions fail to glue.” It is:

1. define a native solution or chart fibration for an exact PDE class;
2. let rigorously bounded residuals generate chart or symbol admissions;
3. preserve scaling, energy, and continuation inequalities;
4. prove confluence and schedule independence; and
5. close estimates with interval or computer-assisted bounds.

Rough paths and regularity structures are strong fixed-grammar controls. Classical 3D Navier–Stokes is not in scope until a validated toy theorem survives them.

### Collatz

[Tao's almost-all theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03562) and large finite verification do not settle Collatz. A disciplined genesis experiment would let failed residue-class or stopping-time certificates generate new congruence, character, or orbit-series tests. It must prove more than enumerating modulus \(2^n\), and a global coercive principle would still be needed.

### Riemann hypothesis

Trace formulas and spectral programs already transform arithmetic test functions into zero-side observables. A failed positivity test could in principle generate a new arithmetic probe, but RH still requires an independently constructed self-adjoint or positivity mechanism and a controlled limit. Random-matrix statistics are downstream evidence, not the missing operator.

### P versus NP and algebrization

Growing the observable algebra does not automatically cross the algebrization barrier. The project must first prove a separation against a precisely defined bounded observer or proof system and then audit whether the argument relativizes or algebrizes. Category-theoretic language alone does not escape a complexity barrier.

### Galois

Nothing here makes Galois theory wrong. The natural relation is Tannakian and groupoid-valued: a finite family of probes reconstructs a current symmetry shadow, while additional representations refine it. The fixed Galois group is replaced, when appropriate, by a relative or pro-groupoid reconstruction—not contradicted.

## 10. Radical but testable hypotheses

These are imagination artifacts, not conclusions.

### Hypothesis 1 — Homotopy response calculus

Every typed question should be answered by its full solution homotopy type. Empty, ambiguous, symmetric, and contractible responses drive different universal constructors. A calculus of these response modes could replace Boolean solvability as the primitive logic of continuation.

### Hypothesis 2 — Dynamic Yoneda geometry

The semantic object is fixed, but the finite probe category grows. Geometry is the changing restricted Yoneda profile, and the complete presheaf is only the limit fossil. “A state exists through its future separations” becomes a statement about which future probes are needed to distinguish representable profiles.

### Hypothesis 3 — Policy-indexed realization spectrum

The quotient-policy category carries a functor into solution spaces. Homotopy-change loci, minimal nullifiers, and persistent monodromy form a constructible or stratified invariant. The next probe is born at a canonical change locus rather than selected from a list.

### Hypothesis 4 — Curved distributive genesis

Resolution is algebraic; observation is coalgebraic. A failed distributive law between repair and observation is not discarded as error but represented by the next higher constructor. Polygraphic and \(A_\infty\)-style controls will determine whether this is new or merely a known cofibrant resolution.

### Hypothesis 5 — Nonrational universal probe coalgebra

The generated probes form a computable coalgebra outside the rational fixpoint, while their coefficient algebra is externally universal on the path completion. This would be the clean coalgebraic version of an externally universal, internally non-finite-state estimator. It is not equivalent to group non-soficity without another theorem.

## 11. What is missing at the deepest level

The missing idea is no longer “more algebra.” It is a theory of **semantic admission**.

A formal system can:

- attach all fillers;
- list all representations;
- generate all critical-pair cells;
- complete all tensor words;
- take all quotient policies; or
- enumerate all future tests.

None of those operations decides why one new distinction becomes meaningful **now**.

The future test or native realization target is therefore not an embarrassment to autonomy. It is the semantic datum that prevents arbitrary self-extension. Endogeneity should mean:

- no external stage counter;
- no future invariant table;
- no hidden universal feature list consulted by index; and
- one stable rule transforming the current certified failure, relative to a typed goal, into the least new legal probe.

The process is autonomous relative to its semantics, not goal-free.

That is the most important conclusion of the sweep.
