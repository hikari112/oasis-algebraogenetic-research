# Algebraogenesis Prior-Art Classification

## Status: broad collision audit, not novelty certification

On 2026-08-05 the project ran 17 intent-ranked Bright Data discovery corpora,
returning 200 results across 199 unique links. Of those, 156 had at least 100
characters of retrieved content. Mirrors, aggregators, secondary summaries,
empty extractions, and off-topic results were excluded from theorem-facing
classification.

Bright Data relevance scores rank retrieval fit. They are not measures of
authority, correctness, peer review, or novelty. The conclusions below are a
skeptical map of nearby mathematics, not an exhaustive bibliographic search or
a priority opinion.

## 1. Main conclusion

Most extensional pieces of the proposed field already exist:

- future-equivalence and canonical minimal quotients;
- finite observations that approximate non-finite semantic points;
- counterexample-driven creation of new predicates or experiments;
- compositional rewrite systems and open-system interfaces;
- sheaf/cohomological local-to-global obstructions;
- discrete holonomy and nonabelian Stokes theory;
- derived-limit and phantom phenomena invisible at finite stages; and
- soficity for groups and graph actions.

The surviving candidate gap is not any one of those ideas. It is their
load-bearing combination:

> A computable, obstruction-generated tower of typed question fibers, whose
> attachment/transport law is forced by semantics or a universal property,
> whose derived coherence obstruction persists under all declared gauges and
> refinements, whose total action/query/certificate cost is fixed honestly,
> and whose induced exact non-sofic action survives the observational future
> quotient while finite external query interfaces remain universal.

No retained source supplied that complete object. This is a corpus-level gap,
not evidence that no prior art exists.

## 2. What must be subtracted

## 3. Classification of the finite transport square

The current obstruction-transport experiment is an exact finite result, but
its mathematical core is conventional:

- split and glue masks are mapped by declaration to opposed triangular shears;
- the two orderings have a rank-one discrepancy and nonidentity loop holonomy;
- the one-use reducer reaches five states;
- unrestricted generation closes to the eight-element dihedral group `D_8`;
- the full atlas log directly answers the displayed order query with zero extra
  persistent bits; and
- all 49 vector coordinates are allocated before either history begins.

Accordingly, it is not endogenous topology growth, Hodge curvature, a
non-sofic action, or an architecture lower bound. Its value is diagnostic: it
isolates the exact datum a successor must derive naturally rather than insert.

Double-category paths and discrete holonomy are the right existing languages
for its path-sensitive part [10, 14]. A cospan, adhesive-square, or sheaf claim
would require additional universal structure not currently present.

## 4. Classification of the universal phantom tower

The universal-phantom construction combines a universal binary stream with a
standard derived-limit obstruction. Let

\[
d:\prod_{n\geq 0}\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\prod_{n\geq 0}\mathbb Z,
\qquad
d(x)_n=x_n-2x_{n+1}.
\]

The stream `U` defines a class in

\[
\operatorname{coker}(d)
\cong
\lim{}^1(\mathbb Z\xleftarrow{\times2}\mathbb Z
\xleftarrow{\times2}\cdots)
\cong \mathbb Z_2/\mathbb Z.
\]

Schochet gives this inverse sequence as the basic nontrivial `lim^1` example
and identifies its derived limit with `\mathbb Z_2/\mathbb Z` [16]. The chosen
universal stream has a 2-adic expansion that is neither eventually zero nor
eventually one, so its class is nonzero. Every finite truncation nevertheless
has an integer primitive by backward substitution.

The associated Stokes statement is an exact weighted algebraic identity. For
the free finite chain complex

\[
C_1^{(N)}=\bigoplus_{n=0}^{N}\mathbb Ze_n,\qquad
C_0^{(N)}=\bigoplus_{n=0}^{N+1}\mathbb Zv_n,\qquad
\partial e_n=v_n-2v_{n+1},
\]

put

\[
c_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N}2^ne_n.
\]

Then

\[
\partial c_N=v_0-2^{N+1}v_{N+1},
\]

and, for the transpose coboundary
`(\delta x)(e_n)=x_n-2x_{n+1}`,

\[
\langle\delta x,c_N\rangle
=
\langle x,\partial c_N\rangle
=
x_0-2^{N+1}x_{N+1}.
\]

Thus the executable's telescoping formula is literally the chain-cochain
adjunction normally called algebraic or discrete Stokes. It is weighted by the
nonidentity coefficient `2`; it is not ordinary constant-coefficient cellular
Stokes, nonabelian Stokes, or Hodge theory. It can be recast using nonconstant
cellular sheaf/cosheaf restrictions only after those stalks and restriction
maps are explicitly defined.

The global obstruction is therefore not failure of Stokes at any finite stage.
It is failure to choose the finite primitives compatibly throughout the inverse
system. **Derived Stokes residue** remains a local project name for this `lim^1`
class, not established terminology.

This packages two established ingredients—a universal shift orbit and the
classical `lim^1` tower—in one executable witness. The package is useful, but
the mechanism is not a novelty claim. It also retains three decisive collapses:

1. the tower and query language are fixed in advance;
2. each stage group is infinite, though finitely presented; and
3. the dense shift carrier is the sofic full shift.

The construction is therefore best described as **externally universal and
integrally phantom**, not internally non-sofic.

## 5. The Hodge/Stokes boundary and comparison target

Classical and cellular Hodge theories begin with a supplied cochain complex,
inner products, adjoints, and Laplacians [12–13]. Cellular sheaf Laplacians and
lattice-valued Tarski Laplacians already extend this machinery beyond an
ordinary fixed graph, so “Hodge on changing data” is not by itself a new
target.

The present `lim^1` class does not force a real or complex Hodge defect. After
coefficient change from `Z` to `R`, the relevant inverse system is

\[
\mathbb R\xleftarrow{\times2}\mathbb R
\xleftarrow{\times2}\cdots .
\]

Every transition map is an isomorphism. Hence this real inverse system is
Mittag-Leffler and has vanishing `lim^1` [16]. Equivalently, the natural
comparison from the integral derived limit to the derived limit of the
realified tower sends every class, including `[U]`, to zero. This statement
concerns the derived limit of the coefficient-changed system; it is not an
unproved commutation of `lim^1` with tensor product.

Consequently, nonzero integral derived coherence alone does not imply nonzero
harmonic leakage. If `P_n` denotes a finite-stage harmonic projection and
`R_n` a cochain refinement, then

\[
\kappa_n=P_{n+1}R_n-R_nP_n
\]

and the one-way leakage

\[
D_n=(I-P_{n+1})R_nP_n
\]

depend on the chosen stage metrics and may vanish even while the integral
derived class is nonzero. They become intrinsic only after specifying the
allowed gauges and either passing to a canonical quotient or proving
invariance.

The minimal credible novelty target is therefore a **comparison theorem**, not
a new Hodge theory. It should construct a target `D(C_bullet)` and a natural map

\[
\operatorname{cmp}:R^1\!\varprojlim A_n\longrightarrow\mathcal D(C_\bullet)
\]

such that:

1. `D(C_bullet)` is defined from declared analytic, harmonic, or
   non-Archimedean comparison data;
2. `cmp` is invariant under the permitted semantic gauges and cofinal
   refinements;
3. the chosen integral derived class has nonzero image;
4. the nonzero image has an effective witness or lower bound; and
5. the construction does not preallocate the future obstruction in its target.

Stagewise finite-dimensional realification supplies no such theorem for the
current tower. Possible coefficient regimes include arithmetic or
non-Archimedean data, while a possible analytic regime would require an
infinite-dimensional complex with a genuinely nonclosed-range phenomenon.
Those are research directions, not conclusions supported by the present
experiment or corpus.

Only after such a comparison theorem exists would “derived Hodge obstruction”
be justified. A later genesis theorem could then require the tower and callable
cochains themselves to be generated by certified semantic obstructions.
Nothing here bears on the Hodge conjecture without a separate bridge to
rational `(p,p)` classes and algebraic cycles on smooth projective varieties.

### 5.1 A first comparison candidate and its limits

The follow-up experiment in
[`genesis-tail-hodge.md`](genesis-tail-hodge.md) supplies one exact but
presentation-sensitive map. For `alpha in Z_2`, let `b^(N)` be its first `N`
canonical binary digits, regarded as a real zero-cochain on the `N`-vertex
path. If `P_N` projects onto the harmonic constant line, define

\[
\mathcal E([\alpha])
=
\limsup_N\|(I-P_N)b^{(N)}\|_N^2.
\]

Adding an ordinary integer changes only finitely many digits unless the tail
is already eventually constant, and every integer tail has zero energy. Thus
`mathcal E` descends to `Z_2/Z`. The universal class has balanced digit density
and exact energy `1/4`, whereas every zero class has energy zero.

This does not contradict the quotient-topology no-go. The embedded copy of `Z`
is dense in `Z_2`, so `Z_2/Z` has indiscrete quotient topology; every continuous
map from it to a Hausdorff space is constant. The nonconstant tail energy is
therefore necessarily discontinuous on the fossil quotient and retains the
ordered digit exhaustion as comparison data.

The experiment also contains a decisive false negative: the noninteger 2-adic
sequence with ones at positions `1,2,4,8,...` has zero limiting energy. Hence
tail energy is a one-sided certificate, not a complete derived invariant. Its
current value is methodological: it proves that a sequence of genuine finite
Hodge projections can expose one phantom integral class when the genesis
filtration is retained.

A bounded follow-up used four five-query intent-ranked discovery jobs and five
first-page keyword searches, returning `46` unique reranked links and `45`
unique search-result links. Retrieval was noisy: the maximum discovery
relevance score was `0.4004`, several searches drifted into generic `p`-adic
Hodge theory, and the literal “tail Hodge” query was largely nonmathematical.
No retrieved title, abstract, or content passage explicitly combined the
standard `lim^1(Z,times2)=Z_2/Z` class, canonical dyadic digits, Cesaro
exhaustion, finite path-graph projection off constants, and a limsup residual
descending modulo `Z` and positive on the selected universal class.

Every ingredient still has a close collision: the derived-limit class is
standard, the path projection is ordinary graph Hodge theory, the residual is
empirical binary variance, invariance under finite or zero-density changes is
near Besicovitch symbolic geometry, and the proposed phase lift is standard
dyadic-solenoid topology. This is no-direct-match evidence inside a small noisy
corpus, not novelty or priority certification.

### 5.2 Primitive escape: a stronger exact comparison

The next experiment replaces the incomplete variance certificate by nested
minimum-primitive geometry. Define

\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{gen}}
=
\{x_n=c+z_n:c\in\mathbb R,\ z\in\ell^2\},
\qquad
(Dx)_n=x_n-2x_{n+1},
\]

and place completed data in

\[
\mathcal Y=\{y:\sum_n4^{-n}|y_n|^2<\infty\}.
\]

Then `D:H_gen -> Y` has range exactly the embedded copy of `H_gen`, which is
dense and nonclosed in `Y`. Consequently the reduced quotient and harmonic
sector vanish in degree one, while the unreduced degree-one quotient
`Y/D(H_gen)` is nonzero and indiscrete. Degree zero retains the
one-dimensional kernel `span{(2^-n)}`. Canonical dyadic digitization gives a
presentation-dependent nonlinear injection of quotient sets

\[
\mathbb Z_2/\mathbb Z\hookrightarrow\mathcal Y/D(\mathcal H_{\mathrm{gen}}).
\]

It is not a group homomorphism because binary carry paths need not lie in
`H_gen`.

Let `K_N(y)` be the minimum `H_gen` norm among primitives satisfying the first
`N+1` projected equations `Dx=y`. Each finite solve is the Moore-Penrose
solution of a finite-rank observation map and reduces exactly to a two-variable
rational quadratic problem.

For canonical binary digits `b(alpha)`, the resulting theorem is

\[
[\alpha]=0\text{ in }\mathbb Z_2/\mathbb Z
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sup_NK_N(b(\alpha))<\infty.
\]

The forward direction uses explicit eventually-zero or eventually-one
primitives. Conversely, bounded nested minimizers have a weakly convergent
subsequence whose limit solves every coordinate equation. Any
`x_n=c+z_n` with `z in ell^2` has `Dx_n -> -c`; a convergent binary sequence is
eventually constant. Thus every nonzero class has `K_N -> infinity`.

This recovers the sparse nonzero class missed by tail variance. Finite changes
of the digit representative have a fixed finite-energy primitive, so norm
profiles change by at most an additive constant. The bounded/escaping
dichotomy therefore descends within the declared dyadic presentation.

The pieces are standard: Hilbert projection, Moore-Penrose minimum solutions,
weak compactness, Hilbert complexes, and dense nonclosed range. The exact
candidate packaging uses them as a **primitive compatibility profile** for a
classical derived-limit class. It remains nonlinear, presentation- and norm-
dependent, uses an infinite-dimensional carrier, and is neither endogenous nor
non-sofic. The proposed escape-rate space is only a hypothesis: equivalent
norms introduce multiplicative distortion, and unconstrained cofinal
reindexing can change apparent rates arbitrarily.

## 6. The exact next theorem target

Define a **question-genesis system** provisionally as data

\[
(H,\mathcal Q,\mathcal C,\mathsf{attach},\mathsf{eval},\mathsf{cost}),
\]

where:

1. `H` is a fixed-alphabet typed history category;
2. `Q -> H` is a fibration or indexed family of currently callable questions;
3. `C(h)` is a finite, locally checkable obstruction certificate;
4. `attach` is determined by a universal semantic repair law rather than a
   chosen matrix;
5. `eval` is defined only for questions inhabiting the current fiber;
6. `cost` charges action symbols, certificate payload, addresses, and suffixes;
7. gauge maps are semantics-preserving equivalences of the whole indexed
   structure; and
8. future equivalence is a right congruence or bisimulation over common typed
   continuations.

The full question-genesis theorem is a later target. It should exhibit one
system satisfying all of:

- a query is ill-typed before a certified attachment and callable afterward;
- attachment induces an explicitly defined weighted chain/cochain pairing and
  finite-stage Stokes identity;
- a derived global class is nonzero even though every finite restriction is
  exact or locally repairable;
- the derived class has a nonzero image under the comparison map defined above;
- equivalently in the current candidate, its nested minimum-primitive profile
  escapes every bounded set;
- that comparison image survives every declared gauge and cofinal refinement;
- the tower fails an effective stabilization condition under a fixed total-cost
  budget; and
- equal-information tapes/programs receive the same payload and addresses.

Only after that should the project seek a semantics-forced action of the exact
non-sofic group. The paper proves a qualitative asymptotic contradiction; a
separate quantitative extraction theorem must turn its expansion, matching,
and repair estimates into one explicit finite `(F_0, epsilon_0)` obstruction
for the fixed group before a finite approximate semantic closure can be
reduced to it.

## 7. Implications for AI architecture

The literature supports a restrained architectural hypothesis:

- ordinary latent-state learning estimates within a supplied representation;
- active automata learning and CEGAR refine a supplied abstraction language;
- a genesis architecture would instead make the **type of a callable probe** a
  state-dependent output of a certified attachment process.

That architecture would resemble a typed, dynamically extended computation
graph more than a probability model. Probabilities could later be positive
functionals on a generated observable fiber, but they would not be the carrier.

No performance advantage follows yet. The mathematical milestone is a natural
availability-growth and derived-coherence theorem, not an optimizer or neural
benchmark.
