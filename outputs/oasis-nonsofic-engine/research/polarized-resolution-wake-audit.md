# Adversarial audit of the Polarized Resolution-Wake

## Status

This note audits the quarantined artifact
[`wild-four-frontier-genesis-dream.md`](wild-four-frontier-genesis-dream.md)
against the typed interfaces in
[`four-frontier-compatibility-scaffold.md`](four-frontier-compatibility-scaffold.md)
and the falsification rules in
[`four-frontier-dream-audit-protocol.md`](four-frontier-dream-audit-protocol.md).

The raw artifact was intentionally allowed to be inconsistent. This audit is
therefore not a criticism of its method. It separates useful generated
hypotheses from statements that fail before any frontier mathematics begins.
No literature or novelty assessment is attempted.

## Executive verdict

The proposed **Polarized Wake Principle is false or empty as stated**. Its
hypotheses omit the compactness, closedness, compatibility, and uniform
coercivity needed to turn finite primitives into a global section. If those
hypotheses are added strongly enough, the conclusion becomes an inverse-limit
or compactness theorem unless an additional causal invariant does real work.

The four frontier routes do not presently share a typed critical locus,
polarization, flux, holonomy, or phantom class. They share a legitimate
architectural role:

\[
\text{finite native questions}
\to
\text{compatible primitive data}
\to
\text{assembly obstruction}
\to
\text{problem-native quantitative control}.
\]

That role is reusable, but it is not one theorem.

The strongest accidental insight is narrower and executable:

> Put a quadratic polarization on **causal repair maps and their comparison
> squares**, not on an untyped collection of questions and primitives. Then
> the failure of two repair orders to agree has an exact curvature-energy and
> signed path-balance identity.

This produces a finite invariant of **quantitative noncommutation**. It does
not turn a high-degree primitive band into ordinary transport, and it does not
solve a local-to-global problem. It is the one part of the dream that can be
tested without importing any frontier conclusion.

## 1. Exact false or type-invalid statements

### 1.1 The state package is not one type

The tuple

\[
S=(\mathcal Q_S,\mathcal C_S,d_S,\mathcal G_S,\mathscr A_S,\Pi_S)
\]

does not specify enough typing to support the later formulas.

- \(\mathcal Q_S\) is called either an algebra or a complex.
- \(\mathcal C_S\) is a second complex.
- A primitive \(p\) is required to satisfy \(d_Sp=z\) for
  \(z\in\mathcal Q_S\), but the source and codomain of \(d_S\) are not given.
- \(\mathcal G_S\) has no declared action on questions, primitives, or
  presentations.
- \(\Pi_S\) is alternately evaluated on questions, primitives, transports,
  holonomy, and formal differences among them. Those objects do not inhabit a
  common additive or ordered space.

The compatibility scaffold avoids this by making the coefficient category,
question type, solution type, obstruction type, dynamics, and bounds separate
fiberwise data. The raw tuple cannot be used until it is expanded similarly.

### 1.2 The primitive tower lacks restriction and invariance data

The expression

\[
\mathscr P_S(z;h)=
\bigl[\{p:d_Sp=z\text{ through }h\}/\mathcal G_S\bigr]
\]

requires:

1. a declared horizon category, not merely integers;
2. restriction functors \(\mathscr P(z;h+1)\to\mathscr P(z;h)\);
3. a gauge action and its higher coherences;
4. a gauge-invariant, lower-bounded cost functor; and
5. a distinction between nonempty individual fibers and a compatible finite
   diagram of fibers.

Without those, \(\delta_S(z,h)\) is not invariant and finite realizability may
mean only that an arbitrarily chosen local test has an answer. The infimum can
also be zero without being attained, and the logarithm used later in the
assembly spectrum can consequently be undefined or \(-\infty\).

### 1.3 The wake attachment is not a pushout

The raw artifact correctly labels

\[
N\!\left(\int_h\mathscr P_S(z;h)\right)
\]

as type-unstable. The subsequent expression

\[
\Gamma(S)=S\underset{\operatorname{ev}}\cup
\mathsf{Wake}_S(\mathfrak D(S))
\]

still uses it as though a pushout category, two attaching maps, and an
evaluation object had been specified. They have not. Likewise,

\[
j_S(\mathfrak D(S))=d_{\Gamma(S)}P_S
\]

equates a groupoid of defects with the differential of one “universal
primitive object.” Initiality or representability would need to be stated as a
mapping property, not as equality between these types.

### 1.4 “Syzygy plus Stokes defect” is not an object

The formula

\[
\mathcal Q_{\Gamma(S)}=
\operatorname{Syz}(\mathscr P_S(\mathfrak D(S)))\oplus\Omega_S
\]

has three separate failures:

- no syzygy construction is defined on the primitive higher groupoid;
- \(\Omega_S\) is presented as a map, not an object in the same additive
  category as the syzygies; and
- the direct sum has no coefficient category.

If \(j:C_S\to C_{S^+}\) is a degree-zero map of graded objects, then

\[
\Omega=d_{S^+}j-jd_S
\]

is a degree-one map. If \(j\) is a chain map, \(\Omega=0\). If it is not a
chain map, \(\Omega\) is its chain-map defect, not automatically a homology
class or a new admissible question. It changes when \(j\) changes unless a
relative or homotopy-invariant quotient is proved. Calling it curvature does
not supply that quotient.

### 1.5 The primitive-nerve \(H_1\) proposal violates the degree gate

The statement that a higher band “becomes a road” because its full primitive
moduli has loops is generally false for the project's relevant degree range.
For a lone degree-\(m\) class with coefficient band \(A\), the universal
primitive fiber has the homotopy type

\[
K(A,m-1).
\]

For \(m\ge 3\),

\[
H_1(K(A,m-1);\mathbb F_2)=0.
\]

Keeping all higher coherences preserves this degree fact. Truncating the
primitive type to a 1-groupoid can manufacture loops by discarding the cells
that kill them. Taking a Grothendieck construction over horizons can also
create \(H_1\) from the indexing/incidence geometry; that is not \(H_1\) born
from the higher band unless a relative normalization and an invariance theorem
prove it.

Ordinary \(H_1\) may still arise from **interaction among multiple repair
paths** in the finite causal resolution 2-complex. That is a different source
and respects the degree no-go. Item 4 in the raw artifact's proposed first
foothold must therefore be removed or replaced by this interactional test.

### 1.6 The respiration and conservation equations are not equations

The display

\[
Q_k\to P_k\to0\to Q_{k+1}
\]

is not a complex, exact sequence, or functorial recurrence as written. The
arrow from zero carries no relation data. The proposed quantity

\[
\sum_r(-1)^r\Pi(\text{questions}_r-\text{primitives}_r+\text{syzygies}_r)
\]

subtracts different types before applying a generally nonlinear
polarization. There is no Grothendieck group, common grading, finiteness
hypothesis, trace, or Euler characteristic.

The reusable residue is only this: an exactification may participate in a
long exact sequence, spectral sequence, relation module, or transgression.
Any conservation law must be derived inside one such typed object.

### 1.7 The holonomy commutator is ill-typed

The formula

\[
\Gamma_{z_2}\Gamma_{z_1}
\Gamma_{z_2}^{-1}\Gamma_{z_1}^{-1}
\]

cannot define causal holonomy. Repairs change the state and are not invertible
endomorphisms of one object. Calling the inverses “comparison morphisms” does
not make the composite type-correct.

The typed replacement is a pair of mutually executable residual repairs

\[
r|q\in A_{\Gamma_qX},\qquad q|r\in A_{\Gamma_rX},
\]

two length-two routes, and a certified endpoint gauge comparison. Their
cellular boundary is a loop in the two-step causal resolution complex. A
serialized interchange 2-cell kills that loop. No reversal of genesis is
needed.

### 1.8 The generated metric is not a metric without new axioms

The separator cost

\[
\tau(x,y)=\inf\{\Pi(q):q(x)\ne q(y)\},
\qquad d(x,y)=e^{-\tau(x,y)}
\]

does not satisfy the triangle inequality from the stated assumptions. A
signed polarization may make \(\tau\) negative. Nonseparable germs can have
\(d=0\), and the absence of a separator requires an explicit empty-infimum
convention. An ultrametric requires nested or tree-like observable partitions;
an ordinary path metric requires a composition/subadditivity theorem. The
honest current object is a distinguishability filtration, not a metric.

### 1.9 The “irrational path” formula does not define a path

\[
\forall h\ \exists P_h,
\qquad \nexists P
\]

says only that each finite problem is solvable. It does not require the
\(P_h\) to be compatible and therefore does not distinguish failure of a
global path from inconsistent local choices. The self-indexed colimit that
follows is a domain-equation slogan until a category, endofunctor, and
existence/fixed-point theorem are given.

### 1.10 The signed polarization law is presently tautological

The equation

\[
\Pi_{S^+}(\Gamma_*x)-\Pi_S(x)
=-\mathsf{Diss}(x)+\mathsf{Flux}(x)
+\langle\Omega,\mathsf Kx\rangle
\]

has no mathematical content if \(\mathsf{Flux}\) or \(\mathsf K\) is defined
as whatever residual makes it true. To become a theorem:

- \(\Pi\) must be a fixed quadratic or convex functional on declared spaces;
- repair maps and endpoint gauges must be specified independently;
- dissipation must be an independently defined nonnegative form;
- flux must come from a declared boundary projection/current, not the
  residual of the desired identity; and
- the equality and sign must be derived and checked under gauge changes.

Section 5 gives the smallest exact version worth testing.

## 2. Why the Polarized Wake Principle fails

The claimed implication is not supplied by positivity, local exactness,
finite flux, and absence of holonomy. Under the natural inverse-system reading,
consider the discrete primitive spaces

\[
P_h=\{k\in\mathbb N:k\ge h\},
\]

with bonding maps given by the inclusions \(P_{h+1}\hookrightarrow P_h\), and
polarizations

\[
\Pi_h(k)=(k/h)^2.
\]

Then:

- every finite horizon has an explicitly computable primitive;
- each \(\Pi_h\) is proper and coercive on its discrete fiber;
- the minimum finite-horizon action is \(1\);
- the groupoids are discrete, so there is no nontrivial holonomy;
- there need be no boundary, so total boundary flux is zero; but
- a compatible global section would require one natural number lying in every
  \(P_h\), and no such number exists.

Thus pointwise coercivity and uniformly finite local minima do not give a
global primitive. The sublevel sets escape with the horizon; there is no
uniform compact ambient set. If “finite-action” is intended to exclude this
example, that term currently has no independent definition and may already
assert the desired compatible bound.

To repair the principle one needs, at minimum, a uniform compactness/properness
condition on compatible sublevel sets and a finite-intersection,
Mittag--Leffler, or comparable assembly hypothesis. Under standard nested
closedness and compactness conditions, existence of a global section is an
inverse-limit compactness theorem. A genuinely new causal principle would
have to derive those hypotheses or distinguish systems having identical
ordinary compactness data. It cannot merely assume them under “coercive.”

The fantasy proof also contains unsupported implications:

- minimality does not make holonomy parallel;
- positive action does not give a uniform positive gap;
- exactification does not bound a primitive's norm;
- finite total flux does not force every transported action to zero;
- zero limiting action does not imply the existence of a compatible primitive;
  and
- excluding zero-cost holonomy says nothing about non-holonomic escape.

The principle is therefore not a candidate master theorem yet. It is a list of
missing lemmas, several of which are the native hard problems in disguise.

## 3. Where the four routes merely restate their conjectures

### 3.1 Navier--Stokes

The “bounded coherent primitive assembling smoothness through the singular
time” is already a continuation-controlling regularity object. Declaring that
every weak solution has it restates global regularity. The dreamed holonomy
inequality has no map to a scale-critical norm, while the sentence that a
singularity migrates into syzygy degree and therefore ceases to represent
unbounded derivatives is a category error. Homological degree does not control
Sobolev derivative degree.

The genuinely missing statement remains an approximation-uniform critical or
stronger bound that preserves the nonlinear term and excludes concentration.
“Vortex stretching curvature” does not supply its sign or coercivity.

### 3.2 Hodge

Defining the primitive geometry to consist of local algebraic correspondences
that assemble into the desired cycle makes nonemptiness or bounded assembly
equivalent to the target. Hodge--Riemann polarization acts on a typed
cohomological space; it does not automatically polarize an incidence nerve or
make its holonomy unitary. A bounded section in an enlarged correspondence,
formal, analytic, or completed category is not a finite rational algebraic
cycle.

The claim that degree may grow without bound through all horizons is harmless
only if the process eventually outputs one finite algebraic cycle. A genuinely
unbounded limiting degree leaves the target category and cannot prove Hodge.

### 3.3 Collatz

A “global descent primitive” is a well-founded descent certificate. Asserting
that every positive orbit has one is Collatz in certificate language. Likewise,
strict inward flux for every nontrivial orbit class either omits nonperiodic
escaping paths or is the required global descent theorem restated.

The proposed logarithmic holonomy is not an exact additive cocycle because of
the \(+1\) term. If \(\varepsilon\) is defined to repair exact additivity, it
contains the missing orbit dependence and risks becoming a tautological log
endpoint ratio. Average negative drift cannot discharge the universal
quantifier.

### 3.4 Riemann hypothesis

The determinant identity is already essentially the complete spectral bridge.
If an operator is self-adjoint and its determinant has exactly the nontrivial
zeta zeros with correct multiplicity and no spurious spectrum, RH follows for
the reason built into the identity. Constructing that identity is not a
consequence of the wake language.

As written, \(\Delta^{1/2}\) is nonnegative and naturally supplies only one
sign of the spectral parameter; the determinant expression also lacks a
defined regularized determinant, operator domain, convergence theorem,
entire-factor normalization, and multiplicity statement. Functional-equation
symmetry is not path unitarity. Explicit-formula positivity over the complete
test class may itself be an RH-equivalent condition.

## 4. Do the four critical loci share an invariant?

No four-way typed invariant has been supplied.

| Frontier | Native “critical” datum | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Navier--Stokes | invariance of a norm or estimate under PDE scaling | scaling exponent of an analytic norm |
| Hodge | equality of bidegrees \(p=q\) | cohomological bigrading, exchanged by conjugation |
| Collatz | heuristic balance of multiplicative height increments | path-dependent arithmetic drift; not an exact cocycle because of \(+1\) |
| RH | \(\operatorname{Re}s=1/2\) | fixed locus of the analytic functional-equation reflection |

The Hodge and RH loci are fixed/balanced sets for declared involutions. A
Navier--Stokes critical norm has zero scaling weight. These three roles can be
placed in a loose schema

\[
w_i:\mathcal X_i\to A_i,
\qquad w_i(\iota_i x)=-w_i(x),
\qquad \operatorname{Crit}_i=w_i^{-1}(0),
\]

but this schema neither identifies the \(A_i\) nor transports any theorem.
Collatz does not enter it until an exact arithmetic involution and additive
weight are constructed. Even then, a zero weight says balance, not regularity,
algebraicity, termination, or zero location.

The proposed assembly spectrum

\[
\Sigma(z)=\limsup_h h^{-1}\log\delta(z,h)
\]

is also not invariant: reparameterizing the horizon changes it, \(\delta\)
may vanish, and the four proposed shadows have different units and comparison
classes. It becomes meaningful only after a canonical directed filtration and
domain-specific normalization are proved.

## 5. Genuinely reusable common structure

After removing the conjecture-specific identifications, the following common
structure survives.

1. **A problem-indexed fibration.** Each native instance has its own typed
   finite-context category, primitive higher groupoids, controls, completion,
   and realization. The fibers need not be equivalent.
2. **A pro-object of primitive data.** Finite solution groupoids and their
   restriction maps separate local nonemptiness, finite compatibility, global
   compatibility, and bounded global compatibility.
3. **Autonomous exactification laws.** Semantic descent, zero/resolved stutter,
   initiality, future-table deletion, and off-family legality remain valid
   cross-domain design constraints.
4. **A causal resolution 2-complex.** Mutually executable residual repairs,
   endpoint gauge witnesses, and certified interchange fillers give a finite
   genealogy-sensitive object. Its \(H_1\) comes from repair interaction, not
   from lowering a lone high-degree band.
5. **A quantitative structure on repair paths.** Native energies, heights,
   degrees, or quadratic forms may decorate the causal maps. They remain
   fiber-specific, but a common balance-law interface can require exact
   transport, sign, and tamper checks.
6. **Assembly and realization as load-bearing maps.** The source certificate
   matters for a frontier only after a separately proved conservative map to
   the native predicate.

This is the quantitatively controlled causal resolution fibration proposed by
the compatibility scaffold. The raw artifact adds one potentially useful
feature: its quantitative control should be tested on comparison squares, not
only on isolated states.

## 6. Strongest accidental insight: polarized repair squares

### 6.1 Typed finite data

Let \((H,\langle-,-\rangle_P)\) be a finite-dimensional real or rational
inner-product space with positive-definite matrix \(P\). Let two mutually
executable repair orders, transported to a common endpoint presentation, be
linear maps

\[
T,S:H\longrightarrow H.
\]

The maps may arise from

\[
T=U\,R_{r|q}R_q,
\qquad
S=R_{q|r}R_r,
\]

where \(U\) is a **certified** endpoint gauge isometry. Define

\[
\kappa=T-S,
\qquad
m=\frac{T+S}{2}.
\]

The polarized curvature action on an input \(x\) is

\[
\mathcal A_\kappa(x)=\|\kappa x\|_P^2.
\]

This is not the homology class of the causal loop. It is a quantitative
decoration of a typed comparison between its two routes. It is invariant under
simultaneous endpoint isometries and changes of input coordinates that
transport \(P,T,S\) together.

### 6.2 Exact curvature-polarization theorem

For every \(x\in H\), the parallelogram identity gives

\[
\frac12\bigl(\|Tx\|_P^2+\|Sx\|_P^2\bigr)
=
\|mx\|_P^2+\frac14\|\kappa x\|_P^2,
\tag{1}
\]

and the signed endpoint imbalance is

\[
\|Tx\|_P^2-\|Sx\|_P^2
=2\langle mx,\kappa x\rangle_P.
\tag{2}
\]

Suppose, additionally, that every atomic repair \(R_e\) has independently
specified nonnegative dissipation \(D_e\) and boundary flux \(F_e\) satisfying
the exact edge certificate

\[
\|R_ey\|_P^2-\|y\|_P^2=-D_e(y)+F_e(y).
\tag{3}
\]

Summing (3) along the two routes and subtracting yields

\[
\Delta F(x)-\Delta D(x)
=2\langle mx,\kappa x\rangle_P,
\tag{4}
\]

with signs determined by the chosen route orientation. Equation (4) is the
finite, typed residue of the dreamed genesis--Stokes law.

It is non-tautological only when \(P,D_e,F_e\) are fixed independently of the
desired equality and (3), nonnegativity, and gauge covariance are exact
certificate obligations. Defining \(F_e\) as the residual makes (3)--(4)
empty. No frontier interpretation is part of this theorem.

### 6.3 What this insight does and does not add

Ordinary obstruction theory records whether the comparison loop is filled.
The polarized square additionally distinguishes:

- a commuting repair square;
- a noncommuting but zero-cost square; and
- a noncommuting dissipative square with order-dependent action.

Compactness is irrelevant in the finite model. Thus a successful experiment
would show that the wake contains more data than “local solutions exist but
global assembly fails.” It would still be standard finite linear algebra plus
a causal typing discipline, not evidence of a new global theorem.

## 7. Smallest exact finite experiment

Use \(H=\mathbb Q^2\) and \(P=I\). Build the same four-vertex two-repair
cellular square for each fixture. Use identity as the only endpoint gauge
unless another gauge is explicitly serialized.

### Fixture A: commuting control

\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
C=
\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then \(CA=AC=0\), so \(\kappa=0\). Serialize the interchange filler; its
boundary kills the causal square. Both path actions agree.

### Fixture B: noncommuting dissipative repairs

\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\frac12
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both are orthogonal projections and hence contractions, but

\[
BA-AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1/2\\
1/2&0
\end{pmatrix}
\ne0.
\]

Set the independently checkable flux to zero and

\[
D_R(y)=\|y\|^2-\|Ry\|^2\ge0
\]

for these projection repairs. For \(x=(1,0)^T\),

\[
\|BAx\|^2=\frac12,
\qquad
\|ABx\|^2=\frac14.
\]

The two path dissipations differ by exactly the signed endpoint imbalance in
(2)--(4). No compactness or limiting argument is present.

### Fixture C: noncommuting zero-dissipation control

\[
R=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
J=
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both are orthogonal and do not commute. Their two routes have a nonzero
commutator defect but preserve \(\|x\|^2\), so all edge dissipation and signed
path imbalance vanish. This is the matched **zero-cost holonomy** control.

Fixtures B and C have the same unfilled-square topology and can be given the
same ordinary \(H_1\). The polarized data distinguishes them; ordinary
homology and compactness do not. Fixture A tests filler exactness.

### Required executable checks

1. Compute all matrices and identities over exact rationals.
2. Reconstruct the cellular boundary matrices independently.
3. Verify (1)--(4) on a spanning set and symbolically for \(x=(x_1,x_2)\).
4. Verify positive semidefiniteness of every declared dissipation form.
5. Apply simultaneous rational orthogonal conjugations and route relabeling;
   the module and scalar actions must transform equivariantly.
6. Duplicate a repair presentation without changing its semantic map; no new
   class or action may appear.
7. Add the certified filler in Fixture A and verify that only its boundary is
   killed.
8. Attempt to add an arbitrary endpoint gauge that trivializes Fixture B;
   reject it unless it belongs to the serialized semantic gauge group.
9. Tamper with one sign, matrix entry, filler boundary, or polarization entry
   and require certificate rejection.

### Posterior criterion

The experiment raises the posterior on the **polarized causal-square
mechanism** only if one constructor and verifier pass all three fixtures and
all presentation controls. It does not raise the posterior on any of the four
frontier conjectures.

The next nontrivial step would be to obtain (3) from one native finite semantic
model—rather than assigning it—and show that its \(\kappa\) detects a feature
not determined by the endpoint complex. Only then should a domain realization
be attempted.

## 8. Frontier consequences after the audit

The raw four-way identification should be suspended. A disciplined research
sequence is:

1. implement and try to kill the polarized repair-square theorem;
2. couple it to the already typed two-step causal resolution complex;
3. test whether a native finite model generates its positive form and edge
   balances canonically;
4. prove presentation and gauge invariance;
5. construct successful and failed bounded-assembly systems with identical
   finite solvability profiles; and
6. choose **one** frontier only after its semantic realization map can be
   stated without the target theorem.

The likely first use is not “one structure solves four problems.” It is a
finer diagnostic separating four causes of failed continuation:

- topological obstruction: no filler exists;
- causal obstruction: repair order carries nontrivial genealogy;
- quantitative obstruction: every filler or comparison has positive cost; and
- compactness obstruction: bounded finite data escapes the native limit
  category.

Those obstructions may interact, but they should not be identified.

## Claim boundary

- The raw artifact contains no proof of any frontier statement.
- The Polarized Wake Principle is refuted under its natural pointwise-coercive
  inverse-system interpretation; stronger readings remain undefined until
  their compactness and compatibility hypotheses are stated.
- A lone high-degree primitive nerve does not produce ordinary \(H_1\) in the
  relevant degree range.
- Equations (1)--(4) in this audit are elementary finite-dimensional identities,
  not new theorems and not frontier bridges.
- The proposed finite experiment has not yet been executed.
- No novelty or prior-art conclusion is claimed.
