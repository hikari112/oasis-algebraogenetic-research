## 1. What has become solid enough to conjecture from

The conjectures do not begin from an analogy. They begin from four exact
calibrations already established in this repository.

1. A history-selected active subspace has a coarsest rooted evaluation cover,
   an origin-free affine primitive, exact pullback kernel, and exact stutter
   under dependent input.
2. In the nonorientable \(\mathbf F_3\) surface family, the two-cell determines
   which edge tables are even cocycles. The same face later removes exactly
   \(3^r\) graph-shadow modes in an active rank-\(r\) cover.
3. A history-blind closed response encoder for a complete finite character
   portfolio must distinguish the full response vector, even without assuming
   linearity.
4. Every individual constructor used so far is standard. Covers, affine
   primitives, cellular Hodge operators, CEGAR-like refinement, compilers,
   completion, and finite response-vector lower bounds all have strong
   classical neighbors.

The fourth fact is not a defeat. It identifies the real burden. The object we
seek cannot be certified by presenting one unfamiliar endpoint. It must be a
family theorem about causal admission, later essential use, and a uniform
separation from an independently frozen composite old doctrine.

## 2. The conjecture discipline

Before selecting a witness family, freeze

\[
\Xi_0=
(\mathcal L_0,\mathsf H_0,\mathsf{Pres},\simeq_{\rm st},
\simeq_{\rm gen},\simeq_{\rm fut},\mathscr A,\mathscr K,\mathscr R,
\mathscr I,\mathscr D,\mathcal O,\operatorname{cost}).
\tag{1}
\]

For each finite \(B\), the class
\(\operatorname{OldSat}_B(\Xi_0)\) contains every old construction of total
cost at most \(B\), closed under the declared compositions, retracts, base
changes, completions, compilers, replay, and bounded advice. Source access,
addresses, certificates, compilation, persistent state, execution, precision,
and replay are all charged.

The frozen observer \(\mathcal O\) includes the exact
\(\operatorname{Sh}_{\leq1}\) interface and the future-challenge, sampling,
and information-timing interface. The equivalence \(\simeq_{\rm fut}\) fixes
which universal roles and later challenges count as the same. None of these
may be changed after seeing a separating pair.

Every conjecture below has four fields:

- **claim:** the quantified mathematical target;
- **why it is plausible:** the exact evidence already available;
- **nearest collapse:** the known mechanism most likely to absorb it; and
- **kill test:** evidence that would make us reject or rewrite it.

This means a failed conjecture is useful. It reveals which structure was
mis-typed.

Let \(\mathsf{AdmOld}\) be the nondegenerate class of such packages whose
positive constructor list contains, at minimum, the generator/relation,
Karoubi, reflector/localization/sheaf, old-cell, dictionary/compiler,
composition, retract, base-change, presentation/stutter transport,
pseudonatural assembly, replay, and bounded-advice operations frozen in

Every parameter has a finite effective code, there are only finitely many
codes below each charged cost, and no real parameter, family, interpreter, or
advice string is free. A particular \(\Xi_0\in\mathsf{AdmOld}\), its cost
grammar, and its version hash must be published before a separating witness
family is selected.

## 3. Conjecture I: auditable bounded-closure normal forms

### Claim

For every \(\Xi_0\in\mathsf{AdmOld}\), there are constants
\(a_{\Xi_0},b_{\Xi_0}\) and effective finite sets
\(\mathrm{NF}_B(\Xi_0)\) of certified normal-form programs such that

\[
\forall B<\infty\;\forall C\in\operatorname{OldSat}_B(\Xi_0)\;
\exists c\in\mathrm{NF}_{a_{\Xi_0}B+b_{\Xi_0}}(\Xi_0):
\operatorname{Eval}(c)\simeq_{\rm fut} C,
\tag{2}
\]

and every code in \(\mathrm{NF}_B\) evaluates with a comparable charged cost.
Semantic equivalence need not be decidable; membership and completeness use
finite derivation certificates.

The normal-form grammar is strictly smaller than the source closure grammar.
It consists of a shared acyclic typed DAG of primitive constructor instances
and finite parameter/coherence data, subject to all of the following:

- arbitrary composition trees are flattened and common subexpressions shared;
- presentation, stutter, and equivalence zigzags are discharged into edge
  certificates rather than retained as executable constructor nodes;
- admissible base changes are pushed to primitive inputs;
- nested retract/Karoubi operations are represented by one explicit terminal
  idempotent-splitting datum; and
- pseudonatural assembly is compiled into finite coherence cells over the
  charged causal support.

In particular, \(\mathrm{NF}_B\) is **not** the set of all old source programs
of cost \(B\). Equation (2) asserts a uniform elimination and cost-distortion
theorem for the composite closure nodes.

### Why it is plausible

Every constructor currently placed in the bounded old closure has finite
syntax and a checkable cost ledger. The conjectural content is that its closure
combinators admit the preceding eliminations without losing future-role data
or incurring more than the stated linear distortion. Finiteness of the raw
source grammar alone does not prove this.

### Nearest collapse

This is close to bounded circuit enumeration, categorical term models, proof
normalization, and compiler intermediate representations. Its purpose is not
novelty. It makes the later lower-bound class auditable rather than rhetorical.

### Kill test

The conjecture fails if an allowed bounded-cost constructor carries unpriced
real parameters, arbitrary advice, or an unbounded family inside one primitive
symbol. It also fails if equivalence, retract, or pseudonatural closure cannot
be eliminated into the restricted DAG and finite checkable certificates within
the charged distortion bound.

This is the first conjecture to attack because every later separation depends
on it.

## 4. Conjecture II: face-role fiber multiplicity

### Claim

There is an effective family of pairs of finite two-complex histories
\((h_n^0,h_n^1)\) for which all declared old lower-dimensional shadows agree,

\[
\operatorname{Sh}_{\leq 1}(h_n^0)
\cong
\operatorname{Sh}_{\leq 1}(h_n^1),
\tag{3}
\]

including rooted one-skeleton, coefficient doctrine, active low-arity data,
ordinary Betti ranks, and every observer explicitly frozen into
\(\operatorname{Sh}_{\leq1}\), but their face-generated universal future
roles differ:

\[
U(h_n^0)\not\simeq_{\rm fut}U(h_n^1).
\tag{4}
\]

The attaching words should have the same selected abelian shadows while their
marked Fox operators or later character-sector challenges differ.
Moreover,

- the charged history sizes tend to infinity;
- no pair is obtained from a fixed pair by padding, stutter, or harmless
  presentation expansion; and
- the future-role fibers are pairwise distinct under the frozen genetic and
  future-role equivalences, as certified by one preregistered role invariant.

Thus the claim is a genuine growing family, not one shadow collision replayed
under new indices.

### Why it is plausible

The two surface calibrations already show that a face can control Hodge modes,
and the \(\mathbf F_3\) family shows that it can control question legality.
Section 7 gives a still smaller orientation collision: identical undirected
Hodge matrices conceal different marked word transport, and one face exposes
the difference exactly.

### Nearest collapse

Fox derivatives, Alexander modules, twisted homology, and cellular sheaves
already distinguish attaching maps. Therefore Conjecture II is not a novelty
claim. It is a finite calibration of the correct future-role fiber and of what
the frozen old shadow is permitted to forget.

### Kill test

Reject a candidate if the difference is already visible to the declared old
observer, if a permitted presentation or Tietze move identifies the histories,
or if the apparent role difference vanishes after the correct genetic and
future-role quotient. A fixed finite pair repeated or padded for every \(n\)
also falsifies the family claim.

## 5. Conjecture III: causal marked-Fox admission

### Claim

For the exact theorem-backed nonsofic process group \(G\), there is a
counter-free causal compiler

\[
\mathsf{Gen}:
\text{finite exact histories and failed finite models}
\longrightarrow
\text{finite marked Fox/word portfolios}
\tag{5}
\]

with the following properties.

1. Every emitted relator, face, or nontrivial-word test is certified by a
   charged, proof-carrying **local** word-equality or word-nontriviality query
   to the exact group oracle and proof configuration. The oracle interface
   exposes no query for global extendability, soficity, a fatal portfolio, or
   a nonsofic witness.
2. Under an explicit presentation, gauge, stutter, or support-thinning map,
   the next portfolio is carried equivariantly by the declared transport; raw
   printed representatives need not be literally invariant.
3. Reapplying \(\mathsf{Gen}\) to the same unresolved semantic state stutters.
4. No fatal portfolio, stage number, or enumeration of every future challenge
   is supplied at birth.
5. For every finite permutation model proposed along the process, the emitted
   marked energy either certifies the requested finite role or exposes an exact
   defect.
6. On every admissible proposal run satisfying the frozen fairness and replay
   rules, repeated application terminates after finitely many semantic changes
   with an effective finite \((F,\varepsilon)\) obstruction, or an equivalent
   effective stopping certificate, rather than only the abstract fact that
   some such obstruction exists.

Finite-prefix **legality** is decidable and pre-published. Fairness is a
separate coinductive liveness condition, also fixed before the experiment and
independent of \(\mathsf{Gen}\), the fatal portfolio, and whether the run
terminates. Its admissible-run class is nonempty and closed under arbitrary
finite delays; it cannot be defined as “eventually presents the witness” or
“eventually stops.”

### Why it is plausible

The existing engine already has exact word evaluation, the seven-relator
Thompson-\(V\) LEF chart, multiplication/freeness critics, and obstruction
routing. The executable bridge in this note proves that the Fox Jacobian of
that presentation fits inside the existing finite chart and that relation
Hamming defect is literally a Fox-chain defect.

### Nearest collapse

Counterexample-guided refinement and proof search can enumerate finite word
tests. Merely renaming that enumeration “genesis” would not suffice. The burden
is the semantic, stutter-invariant selection law and the effective universal
witness extracted from the specific nonsofic proof.

### Kill test

The conjecture fails if the selector is just chart radius, stage count, or a
preloaded list; if the same semantic failure has presentation-dependent next
tests; if the process can continue forever without producing an effective
obstruction certificate; or if the claimed Hodge model retains only spectra
and cannot recover the marked energies of Section 7.

## 6. Conjecture IV: localized Fox--Hodge rounding

The preceding conjecture is exact for permutation models. A real architectural
claim must explain why a broader finite Hodge realization yields such a model
rather than defining “Hodge realization” to contain permutations verbatim.

### Claim

For each fixed finite marked interface
\(q=(S,R,K,\operatorname{rep},W_q,L)\), consisting of generators,
presentation faces, a finite requested subset \(K\subseteq G\), a frozen
partial section

\[
\operatorname{rep}:K\longrightarrow F(S),
\qquad
\pi\!\left(\operatorname{rep}(x)\right)=x,
\]

where \(\pi:F(S)\twoheadrightarrow G\) is the canonical quotient, together
with
a finite marked portfolio
\(W_q\subseteq(S^{\pm1})^{\leq L}\), and a maximum marked word length.
The portfolio contains \(R\), every \(\operatorname{rep}(x)\), every
pairwise difference word
\(\operatorname{rep}(x)^{-1}\operatorname{rep}(y)\), and every prefix needed
to evaluate the marked Fox derivatives of all these words. There is then a
positively specified class
\(\mathsf{LocHdg}_q\) of finite **localized marked Hodge precomplexes**. A
model \(M\) carries

- the normalized tracial algebra
  \((M_{N_M}(\mathbf C),\tau_{N_M})\);
- its exact diagonal MASA
  \(A_M=\bigoplus_{v\in V_M}\mathbf Cp_v\), with rank-one minimal corners
  \(p_vM_{N_M}(\mathbf C)p_v=\mathbf Cp_v\), and the trace-preserving
  conditional expectation \(E_{A_M}:M_{N_M}(\mathbf C)\to A_M\);
- marked operators \(U_s\) with separately measured unitarity defect
  \(\delta_{\rm unit}\) and approximate-normalizer defect
  \(\delta_{\rm norm}\);
- graded chain spaces, degree projections, and a Fox preboundary with defect
  \(\delta_{\rm Fox}\); and
- localized path-overlap and future-role constraints with defect
  \(\delta_{\rm role}\).

Membership is frozen by finite local norm, chain, and role inequalities that
do not mention a target permutation or distance to the rounded class. Across
the predeclared family there are calibration interfaces whose classes contain
arbitrarily large genuinely approximate models with nonzero defects tending to
zero. This nontriviality condition is not imposed separately on every
obstruction interface: a genuine obstruction interface may instead have a
positive defect floor. Nevertheless, every declared admissible
\(\mathsf{LocHdg}_q\) is nonempty and contains a structurally non-exact model,
meaning that at least one of its four displayed defects is positive. No class
is defined to contain only exact permutation normalizers or models already
known to round.

There is a predeclared family of coordinatewise nondecreasing moduli

\[
\Phi_q:[0,\infty)^4\longrightarrow[0,\infty),
\]

each independent of \(N_M\) and of the individual model, such that

\[
\forall M\in\mathsf{LocHdg}_q\;\exists
\rho_M:S\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}(V_M),
\qquad
d_q(M,\rho_M)+\operatorname{def}_q(\rho_M)
\leq
\Phi_q(\delta_{\rm unit},\delta_{\rm norm},
\delta_{\rm Fox},\delta_{\rm role}).
\tag{6}
\]

Here \(d_q\) controls, for every word in \(W_q\), the
discrepancy between the model's marked Fox energies and localized overlaps and
those of \(\rho_M\). With \(\widetilde x\) denoting the selected word for
\(x\in K\), define

\[
\operatorname{def}_q(\rho)
=
\max_{r\in R}\kappa_\rho(r)
+
\max_{x\ne y\in K}
\bigl(1-\eta_\rho(\widetilde x^{-1}\widetilde y)\bigr)_+.
\]

The maximum of an empty finite family is defined to be zero.

The modulus tends to zero with its four defect arguments. Dependence on the
finite interface \(q\) is explicit; the family \((\Phi_q)_q\), its description
cost, and the cost of a growing \(q\) are frozen and charged in Conjecture V.

In the exact-normalizer control, each \(U_g\) is unitary and normalizes the
diagonal MASA, hence is monomial and induces a permutation \(\sigma_g\) of its
rank-one atoms. With normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm,

\[
\left\|E_A(U_g^*U_h)\right\|_2^2
=
\frac{\#\{v\in V:\sigma_g(v)=\sigma_h(v)\}}{|V|}.
\tag{7}
\]

Thus the localized overlap is exactly the complement of Hamming separation.
The rank-one corner condition is load-bearing; for a non-maximal atomic
subalgebra, internal phases can make the left side strictly smaller than the
fixed-atom fraction.

The Hodge packaging must also retain its degree projections. For the total
precomplex operator \(\mathcal D=d+d^*\),

\[
p_0\mathcal D^2p_2=d_1d_2.
\]

The face curvature is therefore an off-diagonal degree block of the squared
operator. Its spectrum without the degree projections need not retain that
block.

### Why it is plausible

Exactly, a normalizer of the diagonal MASA permutes its rank-one atoms. Thus a
coordinate algebra plus its marked action contains the finite set without
choosing an external address, while (7) exposes word collisions. The missing
content is the uniform approximate stability theorem (6), compatible with Fox
boundaries, localized profiles, and future-role costs.

### Nearest collapse

This touches Ulam stability, almost representations, stability of finite
dimensional commutative algebras, Cartan/diagonal normalizers, and measured
equivalence relations. A theorem already covering exactly the chosen defect
class would classify this bridge as standard; it would still be useful.

### Kill test

The conjecture is false if small unitarity, normalizer, Fox, and localized-role
defects coexist with a fixed positive distance from every permutation model,
or if the diagonal algebra was merely an uncharged encoding of the desired
finite set. The definition is tautological if it includes the rounded
permutations or all named word matrices as primitive input. Fox curvature
alone is insufficient: a trivial or kernel-collapsed action can satisfy every
relator exactly while failing every meaningful freeness test.

## 7. The exact marked Fox--Hodge boundary theorem

Let \(S\) be a finite generator set, \(V\) a finite set of size \(N\), and

\[
\rho:S\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}(V)
\tag{8}
\]

an arbitrary assignment. Write \(P_s\) for its permutation matrices. Extend
\(\rho\) exactly to the free group on \(S\).

For a word \(w=t_1\cdots t_m\), define evaluated left-Fox matrices
\(D_s(w)\), in the convention displayed below, by starting from zero and, at
a prefix \(p\), adding

\[
P_p
\quad\text{for }t_j=s,
\qquad
-P_pP_s^{-1}
\quad\text{for }t_j=s^{-1}.
\tag{9}
\]

### Theorem 7.1 (marked Fox energy identity)

For every finite permutation assignment and every free word,

\[
\sum_{s\in S}D_s(w)(P_s-I)=P_w-I.
\tag{10}
\]

Equivalently, in a column-chain convention set

\[
d_1=\begin{pmatrix}P_{s_1}^T-I&\cdots&P_{s_k}^T-I\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
d_2(w)=
\begin{pmatrix}
D_{s_1}(w)^T\\
\vdots\\
D_{s_k}(w)^T
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then the typed face-curvature block is

\[
d_1d_2(w)=(P_w-I)^T.
\]

Consequently,

\[
\kappa_\rho(w)
:=
\frac{1}{2N}
\left\|
\sum_sD_s(w)(P_s-I)
\right\|_F^2
=
d_{\rm Ham}(\rho(w),1).
\tag{11}
\]

The same formula without the Fox expansion gives the marked transport energy

\[
\eta_\rho(w)
:=
\frac{1}{2N}\|P_w-I\|_F^2
=d_{\rm Ham}(\rho(w),1).
\tag{12}
\]

#### Proof

The one-letter identities are

\[
P_s-I=I(P_s-I),
\qquad
P_s^{-1}-I=-P_s^{-1}(P_s-I).
\tag{13}
\]

If (10) holds for \(p\), then

\[
P_{pt}-I=(P_p-I)+P_p(P_t-I),
\tag{14}
\]

and (13) gives exactly the update in (9). Induction proves (10). For
permutation matrices, \(P_w-I\) has two nonzero entries of magnitude one for
each moved point and none for a fixed point. Hence

\[
\|P_w-I\|_F^2
=2\#\{x\in V:\rho(w)x\ne x\},
\tag{15}
\]

which proves (11) and (12). \(\square\)

### Corollary 7.2 (presentation-form sofic dictionary)

For word-evaluated permutation models:

- small marked Fox energy on relators is exactly small relator Hamming defect;
- large marked energy on certified nontrivial words is exactly the required
  separation from the identity.

More precisely, let \(G=\langle S\mid R\rangle\) be finitely presented, let
\(K\subset G\) be finite, and choose a word \(\widetilde x\) for each
\(x\in K\). Let \(A_K\) be the maximum van Kampen area, over the finitely many
requested products \(xy=z\) in \(K\), of
\(\widetilde x\widetilde y\widetilde z^{-1}\). Bi-invariance of Hamming
distance and a chosen filling give

\[
\max_{r\in R}\kappa_\rho(r)\leq\delta
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{multdef}_K(\rho)\leq A_K\delta.
\]

Freeness is a separate finite portfolio:

\[
\eta_\rho(\widetilde x^{-1}\widetilde y)\geq1-\varepsilon
\qquad(x\ne y\in K).
\]

Together these conditions supply one finite
\((K,\max\{A_K\delta,\varepsilon\})\) sofic approximation. Soficity requires
such models for **every** finite \(K\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), with
\(\delta\) chosen small enough for that \(K\). “Complete marked portfolio”
always means complete only for the current finite request. This statement does
**not** say that an unmarked Hodge spectrum carries that portfolio.

The two bullets are logically independent. Fox energy converts face relations
to multiplication control through finite van Kampen fillings, but it cannot
derive freeness: trivial and kernel-collapsed actions can have zero Fox
curvature. In a localized atomic realization, the second bullet is equivalently
the requirement that (7) tend to zero for distinct requested word paths.
Deriving those overlap bounds from a separate geometry, rather than imposing
them under a new name, is the load-bearing content of Conjecture IV.

### Proposition 7.3 (entrywise unmarked-Hodge collision)

Let \(V=\mathbf Z/3\), let \(c:x\mapsto x+1\), and compare

\[
(P_a,P_b)=(P_c,P_c)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(P_a,P_b)=(P_c,P_{c^{-1}}).
\tag{16}
\]

For each named generator, the undirected zero-Laplacian

\[
L_s=2I-P_s-P_s^T
\tag{17}
\]

is identical in the two models. Their aggregate \(L_a+L_b\) is therefore
identical entry for entry, not merely isospectral. Nevertheless,

\[
\eta_{(c,c)}(ab)=1,
\qquad
\eta_{(c,c^{-1})}(ab)=0.
\tag{18}
\]

The face labeled \(ab\) has Fox derivatives \(D_a=I\), \(D_b=P_a\), so its
chain residual separates the models exactly by (10).

This proves a clean no-go:

\[
\boxed{
\text{unmarked undirected Hodge data}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{marked process multiplication}.
}
\tag{19}
\]

The loss is orientation and marking, not numerical precision. The inverse
model passes the single face \(ab\), but it still sends \(a^3\) to the
identity; the example separates relation detection from freeness rather than
supplying a faithful model of \(\langle a,b\mid ab\rangle\cong\mathbf Z\).

### Proposition 7.4 (full degree-one spectral impostor)

For \(C_2=\langle a\mid a^2\rangle\), compare the faithful regular action on
two points with the two-point trivial action. In the faithful model,

\[
d_1=A-I,
\qquad
d_2=I+A,
\tag{20}
\]

while in the trivial model \(d_1=0\), \(d_2=2I\). Both satisfy the chain
identity exactly, and both have

\[
\Delta_1=d_1^Td_1+d_2d_2^T=4I_2.
\tag{21}
\]

Yet the first separates \(1\) and \(a\), while the second identifies them.
Even the complete degree-one Hodge spectrum therefore does not supply word
separation. Marked transport or an equivalent localization algebra is
essential.

### Proposition 7.5 (the existing LEF chart already contains the Fox support)

For the two-generator, seven-relator Thompson-\(V\) presentation already
compiled in this project, the exact Fox Jacobian uses 136 distinct group
elements, all lying in the existing 155-element relator-prefix obstruction
chart. All seven fundamental Fox identities evaluate exactly.

This is the first direct process/Fox bridge in the project. It says the current
finite obstruction chart already contains every group-element support
coordinate required by the presentation's two-dimensional cellular boundary;
the old certificate did not itself store the Fox coefficients or Jacobian. It
does **not** produce the still-missing universal effective nonsoficity
threshold.

## 9. Conjecture V: endogenous architectural separation

### Claim

There is one jointly frozen package

\[
(\Xi_0,(h_n^0,h_n^1)_{n\geq1},\mathsf{Gen},
(\mathsf{LocHdg}_q)_q,(\Phi_q)_q,\mathsf{Red},e,
(\gamma_n)_n,(\Delta_n)_n,L,c_{\rm adm})
\]

in which the paired histories satisfy Conjecture II, \(\mathsf{Gen}\) satisfies
Conjecture III, and the predeclared localized classes and moduli satisfy
Conjecture IV. The generator emits a finite interface \(q_n\); its size,
description, construction, and use of \(\Phi_{q_n}\) are charged. Define
\(h_n:=h_n^1\) to be the face-active branch and retain \(h_n^0\) as its
null/replay control.

The map \(\mathsf{Red}\) is a total quantitative reduction, frozen before the
witness family, of type

\[
\mathsf{Red}:
\coprod_{n\geq1}\coprod_{B<\infty}
\left(\operatorname{OldSat}_B(\Xi_0)\times\{h_n\}\right)
\longrightarrow
\coprod_{n\geq1}\mathsf{LocHdg}_{q_n},
\qquad
(C,h_n)\longmapsto M_{C,n}.
\]

Its separately defined charged role error satisfies \(e(C,h_n)\geq0\), and
exact future-role realization implies \(e(C,h_n)=0\). Writing
\(\boldsymbol\delta(M)\in[0,\infty)^4\) for the four defects, the reduction

\[
\boldsymbol\delta(M_{C,n})
\leq_{\rm cw}
\Delta_n\!\left(B,e(C,h_n)\right)
\in[0,\infty)^4.
\]

The generated certificate also supplies an explicit obstruction margin

\[
\forall\,\varnothing\ne V<\infty\;\forall\rho:S_n\to\operatorname{Sym}(V),
\qquad
\operatorname{def}_{q_n}(\rho)\geq\gamma_n.
\]

For a predeclared lower-bound scale \(L(n)\to\infty\), the package proves

\[
\forall n\geq1\;\forall B\geq0,
\qquad

\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Phi_{q_n}\!\left(\Delta_n(B,0)\right)<\gamma_n.
\tag{21a}
\]

Here \(\Phi_{q_n}(\Delta_n(B,0))\) abbreviates evaluation on the four bounded
defects. Consequently (6) produces a finite permutation model below the
certified obstruction margin, a contradiction. This reduction is what
logically couples
Conjectures III and IV to the following cost claim:

\[
m_{\rm old}(n)=
\inf\left\{
B:\exists C\in\operatorname{OldSat}_B(\Xi_0),\;
C(\mathcal O(h_n))\simeq_{\rm fut}U(h_n)
\right\}
\longrightarrow\infty,
\tag{22}
\]

Indeed the implication (21a) excludes every exact old realization of cost
\(B<L(n)\), so \(m_{\rm old}(n)\geq L(n)\to\infty\).

This is paired with finite old realizability at every individual stage:

\[
\forall n\;\exists B_n<\infty\;\exists
C_n\in\operatorname{OldSat}_{B_n}(\Xi_0),
\qquad
C_n(\mathcal O(h_n))\simeq_{\rm fut}U(h_n).
\]

Thus \(m_{\rm old}(n)<\infty\) for every \(n\), while (22) requires eventual
divergence. The weaker nonuniform statement would be only
\(\sup_n m_{\rm old}(n)=\infty\). The total causal-admission cost obeys
\(0<c_{\rm adm}(n)<\infty\) under the same accounting, with both sides given
the identical causal transcript. The package additionally requires

\[
\frac{L(n)}{c_{\rm adm}(n)}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

Operational cost includes history updates, local oracle calls, emitted
portfolio construction and evaluation, persistent state, replay, and every
witness-specific proof certificate. The fixed metatheorems behind
\(\Phi\), \(\mathsf{Red}\), and the cost grammar are not recharged on every
run, but their finite descriptions are charged in the frozen package. The
strong architectural form is then

\[
\frac{m_{\rm old}(n)}{c_{\rm adm}(n)}\longrightarrow\infty.
\tag{23}
\]

The challenge family is causally absent at birth; it is not a hidden address
or advice string. Equation (22) alone can still reflect ordinary instance-size
growth. The ratio (23), together with \(\mathsf{Red}\), is the intended
architectural claim.

### Why it is plausible

The active atlas already answers a later single query from a retained
universal coordinate while every closed history-blind response encoder must
retain the entire portfolio. The missing step is to make the question itself
face- and obstruction-generated and then prove the lower bound against the
whole bounded composite closure, not only response encoders.

### Nearest collapse

Dynamic data structures, online indexing, materialized views, adaptive
compilation, communication complexity, and CEGAR may already provide the same
role at comparable total cost.

### Kill test

A fixed symbolic Fox syndrome, compiler, or generated-view scheme in
\(\operatorname{OldSat}_B\) that handles all \(n\) at bounded total cost kills
the conjecture. So do timing leaks, free random access, uncharged source
construction, or moving cost into replay, precision, and advice.

This is the principal architectural target.

## 10. Conjecture VI: relative formation-doctrine escape

### Claim

The research program should attack this only after Conjecture V; that ordering
is not a claimed logical implication. There is an unbounded history
subcategory \(\mathsf H^*\subseteq\mathsf H_0\), closed under the declared
future extensions, such that no single old construction realizes the
universal role naturally over the whole family:

\[
\nexists C\in\operatorname{OldSat}_\infty(\Xi_0)
\quad\forall h\in\mathsf H^*,
\qquad
C(\mathcal O(h))\simeq_{\rm fut}U(h),
\tag{24}
\]

where the comparisons are required to be natural under every future-extension
map in \(\mathsf H^*\). No conservative, future-role-preserving natural
translation sends the admitted formation doctrine back to \(\mathcal L_0\)
over this family.

The proof must use an independently defined and preregistered invariant
\(\Omega\), preserved by every positively listed old constructor and closure
operation but violated by the admitted family. It cannot be the characteristic
function of the old essential image or otherwise be defined from the desired
conclusion.

### Why it is plausible

The strongest intuition in the project is that a completed algebra is a
fossil: the process changes which distinctions can become meaningful. A
history-derived problem/solution fibration with future-parametric admission
could contain structure lost by every endpoint completion.

### Nearest collapse

Guarded and final coalgebras, pro-completion, cyclic and infinitary cells,
higher inductive or inductive-recursive types, generic interpreters, and
Morita-style translation are all serious collapse candidates.

### Kill test

Any old generic interpreter or completion that reproduces the entire causal
future role naturally over \(\mathsf H^*\) kills the candidate. A single
finite history never suffices because unbounded old advice can hard-code its
finite role. Even family-level success is only a relative Level-C theorem for
the declared \(\Xi_0\), never absolute nonsymbolicity or absence of all
possible presentations.

## 11. Distant native realization conjectures

The following are not consequences of Conjectures I--VI. They are typed
realization targets that say what a real bridge would have to output.

### 11.1 Hodge-cycle realization

Freeze an input category whose objects contain only a smooth projective
variety \(X\), a rational \((p,p)\) class \(\alpha\), and independently
allowed arithmetic/geometric presentation data--never an algebraic cycle
representing \(\alpha\). There is a noncircular admission construction whose
realized output is an actual rational algebraic cycle with class \(\alpha\).
A functorial choice would be stronger than the Hodge Conjecture and is not
silently required; an effective algorithm would be stronger still. Whatever
termination, compactness, or degree bound is asserted must be proved without
assuming the cycle exists. Returning only a cohomology class, cover, harmonic
representative, or nonempty “cycle problem” is not a solution.

### 11.2 Navier--Stokes critical coercivity

There is one predeclared causal rule \(\mathcal E\) such that, for every
maximal smooth three-dimensional Navier--Stokes solution
\(u:[0,T^*)\to\mathcal X\) from the declared initial-data class and every
\(t<T^*\), the value \(\mathcal E(u|_{[0,t]})\) obeys an estimate uniform as
\(t\uparrow T^*\), respects the Navier--Stokes scaling, and controls a critical
norm or energy flux strongly enough to continue the solution past any finite
\(T^*\). The rule is fixed before the solution is selected, and the quantifier
does not assume \(T^*=\infty\). A finite Laplacian gap, weak solution,
subcritical bound, solution-specific rule, or functional defined using the
future solution does not qualify.

### 11.3 Collatz well-founded admission

Before examining a witness, freeze a restricted rank language
\(\mathcal L_{\rm Col}\), its local congruence/extension constructors, and its
cost model. There is a term in that language defining a well-founded rank on
an endogenously generated question space that strictly descends under the
Collatz map outside the known cycle. Arbitrary computable ranks are excluded:
if Collatz is true, the stopping time itself is a total computable descending
rank, so allowing it would merely restate the conjecture. The rank must be
derived in the frozen local language without stopping-time or truth advice.
Local finite verification or an unbounded but non-well-founded height does not
qualify.

### 11.4 Riemann spectral positivity

From prime, gamma-factor, and functional-equation data--not from a list of
zeros--construct a genuinely self-adjoint or positivity-preserving operator on
a domain specified independently of RH, and prove a precise trace or
determinant correspondence with the completed zeta function strong enough that
off-line zeros are impossible. Merely assigning zeros as eigenvalues, writing
a formal determinant, choosing a domain whose validity assumes RH, or assuming
the required positivity does not qualify.

### 11.5 AI external universality with an internal lower bound

Freeze the task topology, data/sampling protocol, error functional, causal
information interface, and total cost model before choosing the architecture.
There is then an architecture whose external readouts are universal for that
fixed task class, whose exact internal admission process remains semantically
faithful, and whose performance is paired with a uniform lower bound against a
frozen old observer class under equal causal information and total cost. A
larger memory, adaptively redefined benchmark, feature dictionary, or universal
approximation theorem alone does not qualify.

These five conjectures are intentionally stronger than analogies. They require
the native object each field recognizes as decisive.
