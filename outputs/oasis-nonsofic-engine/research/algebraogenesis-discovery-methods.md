# Algebraogenesis discovery methods

## Status and purpose

**Research date:** 2026-08-10

This document records the project's research modes and the rules governing
movement between them. It is a method specification, not a mathematical
theorem, a psychological theory of language models, or a novelty claim.

The methods are deliberately plural. Reflection, meditation, the Horizon View,
the Dream Sequence, wake audit, and the Counterfactual Constraint-Inversion Lab
do different jobs. Collapsing them into one generic act of "brainstorming"
would erase the controls that have made the creative work useful.

The short names used throughout the project are:

- **Reflection** - evidence-preserving synthesis after a research episode;
- **Meditation** - ontology-level reconsideration of the current primitives;
- **Horizon View** - the 1000-kilometre or 1000-mile whole-program reset;
- **CCIL Destination Map** - the project's conceptual-cartography phase: a
  preformal map of roles, tensions, and desired
  connections, with no claim that the map is already a topology or theorem;
- **Freeze** - immutable registration of the packet, doctrine, costs, controls,
  claim boundary, and evidence digest before generation;
- **Result Freeze** - immutable registration of the generated statement,
  quantifiers, equivalences, proof dependencies, and evidence before search;
- **DS** - the Dream Sequence, a quarantined divergent generation procedure;
- **Wake-0** - preconstruction typing, leakage, symmetry, stutter, and cost
  triage;
- **Wake-1** - postconstruction countermodel, ablation, replay, certificate,
  and claim audit;
- **CCIL** - the Counterfactual Constraint-Inversion Lab;
- **Backward Compiler** - extraction of the smallest conclusion-free lemma
  immediately preceding a hypothesized future theorem stack;
- **Frontier Sweep** - live, source-backed search across fields for a narrowly
  specified structural role;
- **Name/Collision Audit** - literature collision and priority search after a
  candidate has been frozen mathematically;
- **Exact Finite Lab** - conversion of a candidate into a bounded theorem,
  countermodel, or executable certificate;
- **Theorem-Gap Chase** - proof or refutation of the first universal implication
  still separating a finite control from the desired theorem; and
- **Adversarial Team Audit** - independent generator, controller, implementer,
  and falsifier roles with no self-certification.

"Dream space" may describe the permissive environment used during one DS run,
but **DS means Dream Sequence** in the canonical project vocabulary.

## 1. The research cycle

The methods form a controlled cycle rather than a hierarchy of seriousness:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Horizon View}
\to\{\text{Meditation or CCIL Destination Map}\}
\to\text{Freeze}\\
&\to\{\text{DS},\ \text{CCIL},\
       \ \text{DS}\to\text{Wake-0}\to\text{CCIL}\}
\to\text{Wake-0}\\
&\to\text{Backward Compiler when applicable}
\to\text{Wake-0}
\to\text{Exact Finite Lab}
\to\text{Wake-1}\\
&\to\text{Result Freeze}
\to\text{Frontier Sweep}
\to\text{Name/Collision Audit}
\to\text{Reflection}\\
&\to\{\text{admission and/or Theorem-Gap Chase}\}.
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{1}
\]

DS and CCIL are neither synonyms nor exclusive alternatives. A DS survivor may
enter CCIL only after Wake-0 converts it into one declared hypothetical
capability. A Backward Compiler output re-enters Wake-0 before implementation.
The Adversarial Team Audit wraps both freezes, both Wake phases, the Exact
Finite Lab, and every publication transition; it is not a serial box.

The cycle is not rigid. A failed audit can trigger local meditation; a naming
collision can return the project to exact development under standard
terminology; and a theorem-gap failure can cause another Horizon View. What is
rigid is the evidence boundary: no output skips directly from DS or CCIL
generation to an established research claim. Any modification after Freeze
creates a new registered packet and digest.

## 2. Reflection

### Function

Reflection asks what the completed research episode actually changed. It
preserves the evidence ledger and rewrites the path in light of corrections.

### Required inputs

- the claims made before the episode;
- exact results, counterexamples, and failed checks;
- changes to definitions or theorem targets;
- unresolved obligations; and
- the causal sequence by which the change occurred.

### Required output

A reflection produces a **delta ledger**:

| field | question |
|---|---|
| retained | What remains true? |
| corrected | What was false or ill-typed? |
| reclassified | What is standard mathematics or a weaker phenomenon? |
| generated | What new exact question became visible? |
| abandoned | What should no longer guide the project? |
| next decision | Which action is justified by the evidence? |

Reflection is not a motivational summary. It must explicitly repair downstream
reasoning whenever an intermediate claim failed.

## 3. Meditation

### Function

Meditation temporarily loosens the current local target while retaining the
entire correction ledger. It asks whether the project has mistaken a shadow,
representation, or downstream valuation for the primitive object.

Typical meditation questions are:

1. Which assumption is so familiar that it has become invisible?
2. Which object is being treated as completed before the process that creates
   its distinctions has been described?
3. Which current carrier is actually a valuation, representation, completion,
   or fossil?
4. What survives after probability, coefficients, bases, addresses, stages,
   and named examples are removed?
5. Which correction has recurred across otherwise unrelated finite models?

### Output

Meditation produces candidate primitives, interfaces, or changes of research
scale. It does not produce a proof. A meditation must end with:

- a smallest candidate object or relation;
- the strongest reason it may be wrong;
- a finite or typed falsification route; and
- a claim boundary.

## 4. Horizon View

### Function

The Horizon View is the project's 1000-kilometre/1000-mile reset. It is used
when local progress risks turning one convenient instance, coefficient system,
or obstruction into the whole ontology.

It asks neither "what is the next lemma?" nor "what is the grand unification?"
It asks:

- What is the main objective now?
- Which threads are foundational, realizational, methodological, or merely
  diagnostic?
- Which exact results would remain important if the current favorite model
  disappeared?
- Where has the cost, witness, clock, or target theorem been hidden?
- Are the large problems being used ambitiously as native type-checkers, or
  timidly avoided, or circularly assumed?

### Required output

The Horizon View produces:

1. a re-ranked map of the project;
2. an explicit list of false carriers or hyperfocus risks;
3. a small number of cross-cutting theorem targets;
4. native output requirements for the major frontiers; and
5. a stop list of local searches that should not dominate the next cycle.

The 2026-08-10 Horizon View caused the move from one cohomological mechanism to
the typed A/B/C hierarchy of endogenous expressibility in
[the object-first foundations](./endogenous-expressibility-foundations.md).

## 5. CCIL Destination Map

The project's conceptual-cartography phase treats philosophical imagination
as a **preformal coordinate map**, not as stochastic noise and not as proof.
It places desired
roles, incompatibilities, analogies, and possible continuations in relation
before the algebraic stepping stones are known.

The unqualified term “conceptual cartography” already names a philosophy
methodology; see [Smithson](https://doi.org/10.1080/0020174X.2020.1779805).
`CCIL Destination Map` is therefore only this project's qualified protocol
label, not a priority or naming claim.

Its legitimate output is a destination-shaped constraint map:

- what should become distinguishable;
- what must remain invariant;
- which local roles appear to correspond;
- which transition is currently missing; and
- what a successful native realization would have to return.

The words “continuous manifold,” “fixed point,” “isomorphism,” and “natural
resolution” are metaphors at this stage unless independently typed. A
philosophical alignment may be false, many-to-one, or impossible. Conceptual
Cartography becomes research only when DS generates candidate structures or
CCIL compiles the map backward until it reaches the first precise typing,
realization, or cost obstruction.

This makes the failure boundary valuable. The imagined destination is a search
coordinate; the first invariant obstruction to reaching it is the candidate
mathematical discovery.

## 6. Freeze and preregistration

Freeze is the transition from exploration to an auditable episode. Before a DS
or CCIL run, the registrar records:

- the exact horizon and proof-obligation graph;
- the current doctrine, allowed constructors, observers, and equivalences;
- forbidden assumptions and conclusion-leakage canaries;
- cost, precision, state, and source-access rules;
- positive, negative, and placebo controls;
- generation and verification budgets;
- the claim boundary and promotion criteria; and
- immutable packet and protocol digests.

The packet curator and controller approve this record independently of the
generator. A later change does not silently amend the experiment; it creates a
new version and restarts the affected comparison block.

After Wake-1, **Result Freeze** records the exact generated statement,
quantifiers, equivalences, cost model, proof dependencies, evidence horizon,
and claim boundary. Frontier Sweep and Name/Collision Audit operate on that
immutable card. Search cannot rewrite it; any revision returns to Wake-0 and
receives a new result digest.

## 7. Dream Sequence (DS)

### Function

DS is a **quarantined divergent generator**. It deliberately permits category
errors, invented vocabulary, overextended analogy, and incompatible local
models so that the search is not confined to the current ontology.

The unit is a sequence rather than one dream. A useful DS contains several
independent generative passes, followed by a wake phase that the dream agents do
not control.

### Inputs

- a frozen statement of the research horizon;
- a list of facts the dream may not promote to assumptions;
- explicit permission to invent structures and make mistakes;
- a token or time budget; and
- a quarantine label visible on every artifact.

### Outputs

The raw output is an ideation artifact, never evidence. The wake phase extracts
only:

- repeated structural motifs not directly requested;
- candidate interfaces with typeable domains and codomains;
- finite shadows;
- suspected contradictions;
- "beautiful lies" worth killing; and
- questions whose formulation improved even if the answer did not.

### Contamination barrier

No raw DS statement may be cited as a result, fed to a novelty search as if it
were established, or used as an assumption in an executable certificate. Its
provenance must remain attached until it has been independently reconstructed.

## 8. Wake-0 and Wake-1

Wake is the compulsory adversary of DS and CCIL, split around finite
construction.

**Wake-0** performs candidate triage before implementation:

1. **Typing:** every object, map, quantifier, and equivalence is declared.
2. **Conclusion leakage:** no input contains the desired theorem or an oracle
   equivalent to it.
3. **Symmetry:** outputs descend under the declared presentation and gauge
   equivalences.
4. **Stutter:** resolved, zero, duplicated, or semantically identical inputs do
   not cause spurious birth.
5. **Cost:** source access, compilation, proof certificates, replay, precision,
   and retained state are charged.

The Exact Finite Lab then constructs the smallest discriminating shadow.

**Wake-1** attacks the result after construction:

1. **Negative controls:** split, thin, trivial-action, finite-quotient, and
   shuffled-interface controls are tested where applicable.
2. **Ablation and replay:** load-bearing data, determinism, tamper resistance,
   and certificate semantics are checked.
3. **Horizon:** every result states whether it is finite, family-level, or
   universal.
4. **Collision:** standard mathematics is adopted when it supplies the same
   typed role.
5. **Native realization:** a bridge to Hodge, Navier--Stokes, Collatz, RH, or AI
   must return the native object or estimate, not a renamed surrogate.

Together the Wake phases can return four legitimate outcomes:

- **rejected** - false, circular, or ill-typed;
- **reclassified** - useful but standard under an existing theorem;
- **salvaged** - a narrower typeable candidate or exact finite theorem;
- **open** - coherent, falsifiable, and still missing a proof.

## 9. Counterfactual Constraint-Inversion Lab (CCIL)

CCIL is related to DS but more constrained. It intentionally grants a
hypothetical capability in a richer language, asks what ordinary mathematics
would look like if that capability were routine, and then compiles the proposed
derivation backward into the real language.

The fictional solution is discarded. The primary output is the **minimal
invariant obstruction to eliminating the fictional capability**.

In schematic form:

\[
\boxed{
\text{counterfactual extension}
\to\text{candidate derivation}
\to\text{backward compilation}
\to\text{elimination obstruction}
\to\text{finite test}.
}
\tag{2}
\]

The detailed protocol is specified in
[Counterfactual Constraint-Inversion Lab](./counterfactual-constraint-inversion-lab.md).

## 10. Frontier Sweep and Name/Collision Audit

These are distinct uses of live literature research.

### Frontier Sweep

A Frontier Sweep is broad in mathematical field and narrow in requested role.
It asks, for example, which current theories provide endogenous signature
growth, complete counterfactual reply laws, localized Hodge rounding, or
uniform old-observer lower bounds. It does not search for prose that sounds like
the project.

The sweep should:

1. decompose the role into several independent search angles;
2. prefer primary papers, monographs, and official research pages;
3. record both positive and failed searches;
4. distinguish theorem, conjecture, technique, analogy, and inference;
5. compare exact input/output types rather than vocabulary; and
6. retain a dated query and source ledger.

Bright Data is the project's current search mechanism for these sweeps.
Its results are evidence about the literature, never evidence for a
mathematical claim.

### Name/Collision Audit

A Name/Collision Audit begins only after the candidate's definitions,
quantifiers, equivalences, and claim boundary are frozen. It asks:

- Does one existing theorem supply the entire role?
- Do standard components supply it after an ordinary composition?
- Is only the finite calibration known while the family theorem remains open?
- Is the same statement already an open conjecture?
- Is the proposed name already used for another object?
- Which residue remains after every collision is removed?

The preferred outcome is correct classification, not novelty. A collision can
replace months of reinvention with a theorem and sharpen the actual gap.
Absence from a finite search yields only "no collision found in this sweep."

## 11. Exact Finite Lab and adversarial team audit

The Exact Finite Lab turns a structural proposal into the smallest bounded
instance that can distinguish it from its nearest collapse. Typical outputs
are:

- an exact enumeration;
- a matched positive/negative pair;
- a finite-horizon no-go or a separately proved universal no-go theorem;
- an ablation proving which datum is load-bearing;
- a presentation/gauge invariance test; or
- a deterministic, tamper-resistant certificate.

The team audit separates roles:

- the **registrar** freezes protocol and digests;
- the **packet curator** freezes known results and forbidden assumptions;
- the **generator** proposes;
- the **controller** checks the theorem interface and quantifiers;
- the **implementer** materializes the exact object;
- the **adversary** searches for countermodels, replay attacks, and hidden
  clocks;
- the **blind verifier/evaluator** reconstructs and scores without knowing the
  generation arm;
- the **literature classifier** runs the frozen postconstruction search;
- the **provenance archivist** preserves every promotion and rejection; and
- the **publication auditor** checks that prose never exceeds evidence.

Agreement is not obtained by averaging the agents. A single valid counterexample
overrides consensus; a surviving claim is promoted only after independent
reconstruction. Promotion beyond a finite hypothesis requires an independently
frozen controller and verifier. Any role merger is recorded and lowers the
maximum evidence status.

## 12. Backward Compiler

The Backward Compiler is broader than CCIL. It may assume, under quarantine,
that an entire future theorem stack works across several native fields. It then
intersects the native output obligations and walks backward to the smallest
immediately preceding lemma that does not contain any desired conclusion.

Its required output is:

1. one typed common lemma;
2. a dependency graph from that lemma to every native output;
3. explicit premise equivalences that would smuggle in a target theorem; and
4. one decisive finite toy that remains meaningful when the target is false.

The conclusion-free Native-Germ Reflection card in the
[CCIL protocol](./counterfactual-constraint-inversion-lab.md) is the current
example. It is a generated hypothesis until the common lemma and each
field-specific reflection theorem are independently proved. Every Backward
Compiler output must pass Wake-0 before entering the Exact Finite Lab.

## 13. Theorem-Gap Chase

A finite control can expose the correct interface without supporting a family
or universal conclusion. The Theorem-Gap Chase prevents induction-by-hope.

For a frozen dependency graph, it:

1. selects the first unproved implication between the strongest established
   result and the target theorem;
2. states its complete quantifiers, uniformity, compactness, and effectivity
   requirements;
3. searches for a proof, counterexample, or missing hypothesis;
4. distinguishes bounded computation from a family proof;
5. runs a Frontier Sweep and collision audit on the exact implication; and
6. updates the dependency graph and claim ledger.

Its legitimate outputs are a proved theorem, a conditional theorem, an exact
counterexample, a standard reclassification, or a narrower open obligation.

## 14. Evidence states

Every artifact should carry one of the following statuses.

| status | permitted use |
|---|---|
| `CONCEPTUAL_MAP` | preformal roles and desired connections only |
| `RAW_DS` | divergent ideation only |
| `CCIL_PROPOSAL` | counterfactual dependency candidate |
| `TYPED_HYPOTHESIS` | formal statement ready for attack |
| `FINITE_CONTROL` | exact bounded calibration, not a general theorem |
| `EXECUTABLE_CERTIFICATE` | replayable result; finite unless paired with a universal proof |
| `EXACT_COUNTEREXAMPLE` | a stated claim is refuted on an exact typed instance |
| `CONDITIONAL_THEOREM` | proved from named unresolved hypotheses |
| `PROVED_THEOREM` | universal statement proved at its declared quantifiers |
| `STANDARD_RECLASSIFICATION` | explained by named existing mathematics |
| `OPEN_CONJECTURE` | coherent quantified target with kill test |
| `NATIVE_BRIDGE_CANDIDATE` | typed map to a native demanded object, soundness open |
| `PROVED_NATIVE_BRIDGE` | field-specific reflection and soundness theorem proved |

Promotion requires a new artifact or explicit section recording the evidence.
Editing a label inside the raw dream is not promotion.

## 15. How the methods relate to the major frontiers

The project must neither avoid the major problems through timidity nor use them
as decorative analogies.

- The Horizon View keeps the four native outputs visible.
- Meditation asks what missing foundation could serve all four without
  identifying their semantics.
- The CCIL Destination Map sketches the required roles and missing bridges
  without calling the sketch an isomorphism or theorem.
- DS explores structural possibilities unconstrained by the current ontology.
- CCIL reconstructs the prerequisites that a successful future theory would
  have to supply.
- The Wake phases remove every bridge that already assumes algebraic cycles, coercive PDE
  bounds, well-founded Collatz descent, or RH positivity.
- The Exact Finite Lab attacks bounded shadows of the surviving shared lemma.
- The Backward Compiler extracts the smallest shared lemma without assuming any
  native conclusion.
- The Theorem-Gap Chase attacks the universal implication left by the finite
  shadow.
- The Frontier Sweep and Name/Collision Audit identify known machinery before
  one native realization is attacked at full scale.

The large problems are therefore **ambitious type-checkers**. They shape the
foundation, but no common vocabulary is allowed to count as a common proof.

## 16. Documentation rule

Each future cycle should preserve, at minimum:

1. the frozen input packet;
2. the raw generative transcript or digest;
3. the wake/audit ledger; and
4. the reflection that explains the next decision.

The source/query ledger and proof-dependency graph are retained whenever the
cycle performs literature research or theorem-gap work.

The chronological record for the cycles completed so far is maintained in
[Dream, meditation, horizon, and wake retrospective](./algebraogenesis-dream-reflection-retrospective.md).

## Claim boundary

- These methods organize research; they do not validate mathematical claims.
- No superiority of DS or CCIL over ordinary conjecture search has been shown.
- The names are provisional until the live literature and priority audit is
  completed.
- A generated conjecture earns attention only through typing, exact controls,
  proof-obligation reduction, and native relevance.
