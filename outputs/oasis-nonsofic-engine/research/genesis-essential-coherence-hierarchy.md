# Genesis Essential Coherence Hierarchy: global emptiness invisible under every proper-subgroup restriction

## Status: policy-derived rank-two essential obstruction and supplied classical higher-rank hierarchy

The finite quotient census produced a stronger phenomenon than failure of one
cyclic detector.  For the trivial-action `C_4` relation policy, the global
coherent-lift obstruction is

\[
u^2v+uv^2=uv(u+v)
\in H^3(C_2^2;\mathbb F_2).
\tag{1}
\]

It restricts to zero on all three proper nontrivial subgroups of `C_2^2`, yet
is globally nonzero.  Within the selected trivial-action `C_4` coefficient
semantics, each such subgroup restriction admits a cocycle lift of the
restricted fixed representative, while the global `E_2` context does not.

This note identifies (1) as the first member of an exact hierarchy of essential
coherent-lift obstructions.  The higher-rank hierarchy is classical
Dickson-invariant and essential-cohomology algebra.  Only its rank-two member
has been generated by the current executable semantics.  The higher members
are candidate targets, not discoveries of new abstract cohomology classes.

More precisely, the generated object is the obstruction `D_2`.  The canonical
precursor `C_2` introduced below is a different cohomology class from the
generated `D_8` precursor, although both have Bockstein `D_2`.

The executable companion is
[`genesis-essential-coherence-hierarchy.mjs`](genesis-essential-coherence-hierarchy.mjs).

## 1. The generated all-proper-view obstruction

Let

\[
E_2=C_2^2,
\qquad
H^*(E_2;\mathbb F_2)=\mathbb F_2[u,v].
\]

For the coefficient sequence with trivial action

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb F_2
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/4
\xrightarrow{\rho}\mathbb F_2
\longrightarrow0,
\tag{2}
\]

the connecting homomorphism is the coefficient Bockstein `beta=Sq^1`.  The
`D_8` extension class used by the current construction is

\[
[c]=u^2+uv.
\]

Therefore

\[
\beta[c]=u^2v+uv^2=uv(u+v)\ne0.
\tag{3}
\]

The three index-two subgroups are the lines on which respectively

\[
u=0,
\qquad
v=0,
\qquad
u+v=0.
\]

Every restriction of (3) is zero.  The exact finite target nevertheless has an
empty global `C_4`-valued cocycle-lift type.  This proves:

### Theorem 1: generated essential mixed transport

The trivial-action cardinality-four quotient of the generated `D_8` sector has
a nonzero global Postnikov class whose restriction to every proper subgroup of
`E_2` is zero.  Consequently no detector consisting solely of cohomological
restriction to a cyclic subgroup detects the class.

This is strictly stronger than saying a chosen axis can fail.  The obstruction
belongs to the interaction of the complete pair of directions.

## 2. Essential cohomology of an elementary abelian context

Let

\[
E_n=(C_2)^n,
\qquad
V_n=E_n^*,
\qquad
H^*(E_n;\mathbb F_2)=\operatorname{Sym}(V_n).
\tag{4}
\]

Define the all-directions product

\[
\boxed{
D_n
=
\prod_{0\ne\lambda\in V_n}\lambda.
}
\tag{5}
\]

It has degree

\[
|V_n\setminus\{0\}|=2^n-1.
\]

This is the top Dickson invariant, often denoted `Q_{n,0}` or `L_n` at the
prime two.

Define the essential ideal

\[
\operatorname{Ess}(E_n)
=
\bigcap_{H<E_n}
\ker\!\left(
\operatorname{res}^{E_n}_{H}
\right).
\tag{6}
\]

### Theorem 2: exact essential ideal

For every `n>=1`,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Ess}(E_n)=(D_n).
}
\tag{7}
\]

In particular, `D_n` is nonzero globally and restricts to zero on every proper
subgroup of `E_n`.

### Proof

The polynomial ring in (4) is an integral domain, so the product (5) is
nonzero.  If `H<E_n` is proper, its annihilator contains some nonzero
`lambda in V_n`.  That factor restricts to zero on `H`, hence so does `D_n`.

Conversely, every proper subgroup is contained in a hyperplane, so vanishing on
all hyperplanes is equivalent to vanishing on all proper subgroups.  For the
hyperplane `ker lambda`, the kernel of polynomial restriction is the principal
ideal `(lambda)`.  If a polynomial vanishes on every hyperplane restriction,
it is divisible by every nonzero linear form.  Distinct linear forms over
`F_2` are nonassociate irreducibles, so their product divides the polynomial.
Therefore the intersection (6) is exactly `(D_n)`.  \(\square\)

The theorem gives a precise hierarchy of interaction order.  Every restriction
to a subgroup of rank less than `n` kills `D_n`; within this
subgroup-restriction semantics, only the full rank-`n` group carries a nonzero
restriction of this class.

## 3. A canonical, but not unique, Bockstein precursor

Assume `n>=2`.  Define

\[
\boxed{
C_n
=
\sum_{0\ne\ell\in V_n}
\prod_{\substack{0\ne\lambda\in V_n\\\lambda\ne\ell}}
\lambda.
}
\tag{8}
\]

Its degree is `2^n-2`.  Because `GL_n(F_2)` permutes the set of nonzero linear
forms, (8) is basis-independent and invariant.  It is the next Dickson
invariant `Q_{n,1}`.

Canonical here means basis-independent and `GL_n(F_2)`-invariant by the
displayed formula.  It does not mean that `D_n` has a unique Bockstein
precursor.  Any class in the affine fiber `Sq^1{}^{-1}(D_n)` is another
precursor, and the current semantics does not select `C_n` for `n>2`.

### Theorem 3: Bockstein generation

For every `n>=2`,

\[
\boxed{
Sq^1(C_n)=D_n.
}
\tag{9}
\]

### Proof

For a fixed omitted factor `ell`, write

\[
C_{n,\ell}
=
\prod_{\lambda\ne0,\ell}\lambda.
\]

The Bockstein is a derivation and satisfies `Sq^1(lambda)=lambda^2`, so

\[
Sq^1(C_{n,\ell})
=
C_{n,\ell}
\sum_{\lambda\ne0,\ell}\lambda.
\tag{10}
\]

Every coordinate occurs in `2^{n-1}` of the nonzero linear forms.  Since that
number is even for `n>=2`,

\[
\sum_{\lambda\ne0}\lambda=0.
\]

The sum in (10) is therefore `ell`, giving

\[
Sq^1(C_{n,\ell})=C_{n,\ell}\ell=D_n.
\]

There are `2^n-1` omitted-factor terms, an odd number, so their Bocksteins sum
to `D_n`.  \(\square\)

For `n=2`, the generated `D_8` precursor is

\[
c_{\mathrm{gen}}=u^2+uv,
\]

whereas the canonical Dickson precursor is

\[
C_2=u^2+uv+v^2,
\qquad
D_2=u^2v+uv^2.
\tag{11}
\]

They are distinct cohomology classes, differing by the nonzero square

\[
v^2\in\ker Sq^1.
\]

Both have Bockstein `D_2`.  By exactness, `v^2` has a `Z/4`-valued cocycle
lift; after choosing such a lift, translation gives a noncanonical bijection
between the two fixed-representative cocycle-lift types.  Thus the generated
obstruction, not the precursor or its cocycle gauge, is exactly the rank-two
member of the canonical hierarchy.

## 4. Global empty lift, local inhabited lifts

Use the coefficient sequence (2) with trivial `E_n`-action.  Put

\[
m_n=2^n-2,
\]

and choose a normalized cocycle

\[
c_n\in Z^{m_n}(E_n;\mathbb F_2),
\qquad
[c_n]=C_n.
\]

and define

\[
\operatorname{CocLift}_{c_n}(\mathbb Z/4)
=
\{F\in Z^{m_n}(E_n;\mathbb Z/4):\rho F=c_n\}.
\tag{12}
\]

### Theorem 4: essential coherent-lift hierarchy for fixed representatives

For every `n>=2`,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{CocLift}_{c_n}(\mathbb Z/4)=\varnothing,
}
\tag{13}
\]

but for every proper subgroup `H<E_n`,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{CocLift}_{\operatorname{res}_H c_n}(\mathbb Z/4)
\ne\varnothing.
}
\tag{14}
\]

### Proof

The obstruction to (12) is the connecting image

\[
\beta(C_n)=D_n,
\]

which is nonzero by Theorems 2 and 3.  Hence the global type is empty.  On a
proper subgroup, Theorem 2 gives

\[
\operatorname{res}_H D_n=0.
\]

Exactness supplies a `Z/4`-valued cocycle \(\overline F_H\) such that

\[
[\rho\overline F_H]=[\operatorname{res}_Hc_n].
\]

Hence

\[
\rho\overline F_H-\operatorname{res}_Hc_n=\delta a
\]

for a normalized lower-degree `F_2`-cochain `a`.  Choose a normalized pointwise
lift \(\widetilde a\) to `Z/4` and set

\[
F_H=\overline F_H-\delta\widetilde a.
\]

Then `F_H` remains a cocycle and satisfies

\[
\rho F_H=\operatorname{res}_Hc_n
\]

exactly.  \(\square\)

The local lifts in (14) are neither canonical nor asserted to form one
compatible family over the subgroup lattice.  The theorem is a local existence
versus global existence result, not a gluing theorem for selected local
solutions.

For any fixed subgroup-rank bound `r`, choosing `n>r` produces a global
obstruction invisible under every subgroup restriction of rank at most `r`.
This is an exact bounded-subgroup-view separation.  It is not a lower bound
against arbitrary bounded features or algorithms: a globally supplied
polynomial can be evaluated directly, and a finite lookup table can detect the
class.

## 5. Optional universal repair and its declared scaling

Essentiality alone does not demand a new representer.  If normalized
`F_2[E_n]`-bar-module semantics are separately declared, however, the
primitive-context construction can be applied to the obstruction class.  This
is a further declared primitive question for the obstruction cocycle.  It is
not the universal representer of the original `Z/4` cocycle-lift functor in
Theorem 4.

Let

\[
\delta_n:B_{m_n+1}\longrightarrow\mathbb F_2,
\qquad
[\delta_n]=D_n,
\]

be a normalized bar cocycle, where `B_*` is the normalized free bar resolution
over `F_2[E_n]`.  The universal primitive-context representer is

\[
\Gamma_n
=
\left(
\mathbb F_2\oplus B_{m_n}
\right)
\Big/
\operatorname{im}
\left(
B_{m_n+1}
\xrightarrow{(\delta_n,-d_{m_n+1})}
\mathbb F_2\oplus B_{m_n}
\right).
\tag{15}
\]

Cohomologous choices of `delta_n` give the corresponding gauge-isomorphic
representers under `F_2`, with the universal primitive translated by the chosen
lower cochain.

It fits into a nonsplit exact sequence of `F_2[E_n]`-modules

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb F_2
\longrightarrow\Gamma_n
\longrightarrow\operatorname{im}d_{m_n}
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{16}
\]

As a sequence of plain `F_2`-vector spaces it splits; the obstruction is
equivariant.

Since `|E_n|=2^n`, the declared normalized-bar encoding gives

\[
\boxed{
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\Gamma_n
=
1+(2^n-1)^{2^n-2}.
}
\tag{17}
\]

Already

\[
n=2:\ 10,
\qquad
n=3:\ 117{,}650.
\]

This explosive exact count is a representer dimension in one declared cochain
language.  It is not invariant under every equivalent resolution or semantic
compiler, and it is not a lower bound on arbitrary programs.

The `n=2` object in (15) is also not the rank-13 integral representer from the
preceding note.  It uses the pushed-forward `F_2`-valued essential class and an
`F_2` bar category; the rank-13 object represents primitives of the original
even-augmentation-lattice cocycle over `Z`.

## 6. Topology, path geometry, and the exact limitation

Let `rho_reg_tilde` be the reduced real regular representation of `E_n`, and
let

\[
\xi_n
=
EE_n\times_{E_n}\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}
\longrightarrow BE_n
\]

be its associated bundle.  The representation is the direct sum of all
nontrivial real sign characters, and the first Stiefel--Whitney class of the
character `lambda` is `lambda`.  Therefore

\[
w_{2^n-1}(\xi_n)
=
\prod_{0\ne\lambda\in V_n}\lambda
=
D_n.
\]

Thus `D_n` is the top Stiefel--Whitney, equivalently mod-two Euler, class of
this associated bundle.  This gives the all-directions product a genuine
topological interpretation.

It also sharpens the path-space intuition.  The obstruction class is not
located between two scalar values or along one irrational coordinate.  It is a
global interaction class that disappears along every lower-rank subgroup
restriction.  Within subgroup-restriction semantics, its nonvanishing becomes
visible only at the full independent-direction context.

But the hierarchy remains finite, algebraic, symbolic, and mod-two at each
stage.  Every `E_n` is finite and sofic.  These classes are torsion and do not
produce rational Hodge classes.  Essentiality is therefore a rigorous model of
local invisibility, not yet the sought transcendental algebra or non-sofic
genesis object.  Moreover, the natural direct limit

\[
\bigoplus_{\mathbb N}C_2
\]

is abelian, amenable, and sofic.  Merely increasing `n` in this hierarchy cannot
produce group non-soficity.

## 7. Generated versus supplied

The preceding process generated:

- the rank-two group `E_2=V_4`;
- the precursor cohomology class `u^2+uv` from the `D_8` extension.

After the trivial-action `C_4` coefficient policy was selected from the exact
relation-policy lattice, the present analysis derived and exhaustively checked:

- the Bockstein obstruction `D_2=uv(u+v)`; and
- global nonvanishing with vanishing on every proper subgroup.

The process has not yet selected that `C_4` policy endogenously.

For `n>2`, the following are still supplied:

- the growth rule `E_n -> E_{n+1}`;
- the complete family of nonzero linear observables;
- multiplication of all directions and the deleted-factor sum in (8);
- the `C_4` coefficient extension as the selected relation policy;
- a semantic higher boundary or prior universal element whose generated
  cohomology class lies in `Sq^1{}^{-1}(D_n)`;
- a rule producing an actual normalized cocycle representative up to certified
  coboundary, rather than inserting the polynomial `C_n`;
- a proof that the higher-rank precursor is generated from the preceding stage
  rather than merely uniformly computable from `n`;
- the rule promoting the empty type (13) to a new semantic dimension; and
- retention of the resulting higher witness genealogy.

The family is externally enumerable.  Supplying `n` or the Dickson formula on
an external tape would not be algebraogenesis.

## 8. Honest next theorem gate

The new target is not to prove Theorems 2--4 again; they are classical
cohomological facts.  It is to generate their input.

> Construct an endogenous typed process whose current universal element
> functorially exposes a new independent binary transport direction, closes the
> observable policy under all nonzero linear combinations, and generates a
> higher class
> \[
> \zeta_n\in H^{2^n-2}(E_n;\mathbb F_2)
> \]
> satisfying
> \[
> Sq^1(\zeta_n)=D_n,
> \]
> without access to an external stage counter.  The output should be canonical
> only up to coboundary and addition of coefficient-liftable classes in
> `ker Sq^1`, unless a further semantic theorem selects the specific Dickson
> precursor `C_n`.

The target must survive:

1. **external-index falsification:** removing a stage counter or prewritten
   basis list cannot stop the process;
2. **proper-view falsification:** every claimed proper-subgroup restriction
   must genuinely admit the lift, not merely report a zero scalar detector;
3. **compatibility falsification:** local lifts must not be silently treated as
   a compatible global family;
4. **class/gauge falsification:** changing a normalized representative by a
   coboundary must induce the claimed isomorphism of lift questions, while
   changing the precursor by a nonzero class in `ker Sq^1` must not be called a
   gauge change; comparison then requires a chosen `Z/4` lift;
5. **semantic-compiler falsification:** growth in the normalized bar encoding
   must not be mistaken for an invariant lower bound;
6. **fixed-family falsification:** replaying a supplied sequence of Dickson
   polynomials is not genesis; and
7. **non-soficity falsification:** essentiality alone cannot certify a
   non-sofic process.

Success would yield an exact hierarchy in which every proper-subgroup
restriction of the selected coherent-lift question is soluble while each newly
completed global context creates a fresh empty coherent-lift type.  An
independent argument would still be required to turn that hierarchy into
non-soficity or an AI architectural lower bound.

## 9. Literature classification

The polynomial invariants in this note are classical.  Wilkerson's
[primer on the Dickson invariants](https://bookstore.ams.org/conm-19) records
the invariant-ring generators and Steenrod action.  Aksu and Green's
[essential-cohomology treatment](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.4425) gives the
standard definition and broader elementary-abelian context, explicitly noting
that the prime-two case is the straightforward classical case.  Gunawardena's
[representation-theoretic Stiefel--Whitney study](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-35.3.539)
is part of the established characteristic-class setting.

Accordingly, no novelty claim is made for `D_n`, `C_n`, essential cohomology,
or the Stiefel--Whitney identification.  The research candidate is the
obstruction-driven semantic pipeline: a generated precursor, a selected
relation policy, an essential empty lift question, and a future endogenous rule
that would generate the next precursor only up to liftable additions.
